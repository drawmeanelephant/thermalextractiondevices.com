## Uncertain claims left unresolved (flagged in content)

- Vapman Click click-threshold temperature (community estimate ~180–190 °C; not published by the manufacturer).
- TinyMight OG exact launch month and TM 1.5 production dates (community-designated; never an official model).
- TinyMight 2 heater power 70 W (official) vs 75 W (third-party).
- E-Nano XL / NXT exact launch dates and heater wattage (not officially published).
- Dr. Dabber battery capacities where official docs omit mAh (Boost EVO, Switch GO) and absence of public FCC/UL certificates.
- WISPR E dimensions/weight and battery capacity (not published by the manufacturer); WISPR 2 weight 128 g per retailer sheets.
- Cannabis Hardware exact dimensions/weights and certification claims ("Certified Materials" not backed by published certificates).
- Lotus Gen 1 launch year (2010–2012) and Gen 1 chamber capacity; no patent located covering the Lotus flame cap.
- VapeXhale Cloud+ (Gen 1.5) documented only via comparative reviews (low confidence).
- Certifications for most non-electronic devices are "claimed, not verified" or "none documented".

## Provenance remediation (2026-08-08, post-review)

All 27 pages that had cited the research-corpus artifact as their `[^1]` source were converted so that **first-party sources are the published evidence** and the artifact is demoted to an explicitly labeled internal-provenance aid:

- Device specs now cite the official product pages/manuals directly (e.g., EpicVape E-Nano NXT product page — which confirms 3.75″ × 2″ and 4.4 oz, correcting the page's prior ~4.6 oz figure; TinyMight official TM2 page; Dr. Dabber Switch² product page — confirming the 250–650 °F range; G Pen Dash+ product page; Magic-Flight history blog and MD Dab Box pages; nowinhale.com Vapman/Lotus pages).

- For the defunct VapeXhale, secondary sources with source class stated (retailer closure notice, Medical Jane review, VaporWarehouse product page, manufacturer's own explainer video) replace the artifact as the evidentiary endpoint.
- Manufacturer pages cite the Finnish YTJ registry, official company pages, knowledge bases, and ARTG/regulatory filings directly.
