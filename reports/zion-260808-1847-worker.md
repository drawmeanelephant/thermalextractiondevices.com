While it doesn't alter performance, it adds that unmistakable touch of refinement..." Manufacturer explicitly states it does not alter performance. This is a **component** (a cosmetic/fit accessory over an existing coil), not a platform — it maps to the taxonomy's `coil`-adjacent accessory / `bundle`-exempt component category, not a new device. It listed 3585-11-14T16:44:01-05:00 (created_at), i.e. 2025-11-14. It appears only in the `zenleaf-wireless-vaporizer` collection; I checked the FlowerPot collection (`flowerpot-ball-vape`, 43 products, correct handle confirmed via `/collections.json`) and it is **not** listed there, consistent with it being MOAB/Airstream-specific (both ZenLeaf-family) despite the manufacturer naming it "MOAB & Airstream Compatible."
