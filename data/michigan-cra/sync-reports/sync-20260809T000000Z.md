## Integrity and privacy

- No street addresses, phone numbers, emails, coordinates, or private Accela/
  Metrc data were retained.
- Legal entity, DBA, brand, license, premises, product, recall, and analyte are
  separate records/fields.
- No demonstration COA or synthetic Michigan chemistry record was counted.
- `ND`, `<LOD`, and `<LOQ` semantics were not collapsed; there are no Michigan
  result rows in this sync.

## Known limitations

CRA's license search is interactive and the periodic licensing reports are
document-oriented. The public sources reviewed do not provide a stable bulk
license download. Laboratory pages expose service and accreditation details but
not a complete official directory. COA access is generally client/portal based.
