# TSAFE-0001 Overpressure & Thermal Runaway Protocol

Standard emergency shutdown procedure for high-flux extraction loops.

## Emergency Cutoff Sequence

1. **Isolate Primary Loop**: Actuate pneumatic emergency shutoff valves (ESV-1 and ESV-2).
2. **Engage Cryo-Quench**: Flood secondary heat exchanger with chilled N2 flush.
3. **Depressurize Vent**: Direct vent effluent to scrubber buffer tank.
