## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: Sensi Seeds
- **Lineage**: [Skunk #1](skunk-1.md) × Afghan Indica Landrace
- **Classification**: Indica-dominant hybrid
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Released in 1990 by Sensi Seeds, Super Skunk backcrossed the classic Skunk #1 line with an exceptional Afghan hash plant to enhance density, resin output, and deep earthy pungent notes.
