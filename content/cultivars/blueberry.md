## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: DJ Short / Dutch Passion
- **Lineage**: Afghan Indica × (Purple Thai × Highland Thai)
- **Classification**: Indica-dominant hybrid
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md), [D-Limonene](../terpenes/d-limonene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Originating in the late 1970s through breeder DJ Short and introduced to European seed banks by Dutch Passion in the 1990s, Blueberry is renowned for its distinctive sweet berry aroma and floral undertones.
