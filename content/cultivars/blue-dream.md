# Blue Dream

## Genetic Identity & Overview

- **Lineage**: Blueberry × Haze
- **Classification**: Hybrid
- **Dominant Terpene Descriptors**: β-Myrcene, D-Limonene, α-Pinene, β-Caryophyllene

> Note on Cultivar Profiles: A strain or cultivar does not possess one permanent terpene profile. Different growers, harvests, curing methods, batches, and laboratories can produce different results. The batch-level Certificate of Analysis (COA) is the factual source; this cultivar page serves as an overview and an index into those results.
