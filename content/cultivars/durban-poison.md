## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: Dutch Passion (landrace selection inbred from South African genetics)
- **Lineage**: South African Sativa Landrace (Durban port region)
- **Classification**: Pure Sativa Landrace selection
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [Terpinolene](../terpenes/terpinolene.md), [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [α-Pinene](../terpenes/alpha-pinene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Imported from South Africa in the 1970s and acclimated to European outdoor and indoor cultivation by Dutch Passion, Durban Poison is celebrated for its unique sweet anise, pine, and spicy terpinolene-heavy aroma profile.
