## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: Sensi Seeds (Netherlands)
- **Lineage**: Haze × (Northern Lights #5 × Shiva Skunk)
- **Classification**: Sativa-dominant hybrid
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [α-Pinene](../terpenes/alpha-pinene.md), [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Developed in the Netherlands during the mid-1990s and named in honor of cannabis activist Jack Herer, this cultivar combines a complex tropical Sativa aroma with dense resin production derived from its Northern Lights and Skunk ancestral lines.
