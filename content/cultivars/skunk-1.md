## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: Sacred Seeds / Sensi Seeds
- **Lineage**: Afghan Indica × (Colombian Gold × Acapulco Gold)
- **Classification**: Hybrid benchmark strain
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md), [D-Limonene](../terpenes/d-limonene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Bred in California during the 1970s by Sacred Seeds (David Watson / Sam the Skunkman) and refined in the Netherlands by Sensi Seeds, Skunk #1 set the global baseline for hybrid vigor, rapid flowering, and pungent musky aroma.
