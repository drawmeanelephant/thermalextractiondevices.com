## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: Widely credited to Kyle Kushman; sold by Dutch Passion (breeder credit is disputed in community accounts)
- **Lineage**: Strawberry Field(s) × Haze (per widely reported accounts)
- **Classification**: Sativa-dominant hybrid
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [D-Limonene](../terpenes/d-limonene.md), [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Popularized in North America and offered in global seed catalogs by Dutch Passion, Strawberry Cough is recognized for its distinct sweet strawberry sensory aroma and uplifting Haze lineage. The Strawberry Fields × Haze parentage is widely reported; the breeder credit is contested, with Kyle Kushman the most commonly named originator and some community accounts attributing the original to another breeder (Jeff Cavanagh).
