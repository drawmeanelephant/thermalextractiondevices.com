## Genetic Identity & Origin

- **Primary Breeder**: Kyle Kushman / Dutch Passion
- **Lineage**: Strawberry Field × Haze
- **Classification**: Sativa-dominant hybrid
- **Common Primary Terpene Descriptors**: [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md), [D-Limonene](../terpenes/d-limonene.md), [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md)

## First-Party Provenance & Breeder Notes

Popularized in North America and introduced to global seed catalogs by Dutch Passion, Strawberry Cough is recognized for its distinct sweet strawberry sensory aroma and uplifting Haze lineage.
