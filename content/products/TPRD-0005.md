# BLOOM Classic and BLOOM Live Vape Cartridges

The CRA named BLOOM vape cartridges in a February 11, 2025 voluntary recall for
MCT oil. The notice lists 15 impacted strain labels, including Blue Dream,
Jack Herer, and White Widow. Those strings are preserved as notice labels only.
