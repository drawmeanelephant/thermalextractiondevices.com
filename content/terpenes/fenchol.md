# Fenchol

## Identity

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Preferred name | Fenchol (fenchyl alcohol) |
| IUPAC name | 1,3,3-trimethylbicyclo[2.2.1]heptan-2-ol |
| CAS number | 1632-73-1 |
| PubChem CID | 15406 |
| Chemical family | Bicyclic Monoterpene Alcohol |
| Molecular formula | C10H18O |
| Molecular mass | 154.25 g/mol |
| Stereochemistry | Two chiral centers; (+)- and (−)-enantiomers both occur in nature [^1] |

## Aroma and sensory character

Camphoraceous, pine, woody, with fresh fennel and citrus-lime notes.

## Occurrence outside cannabis

- Sweet fennel (*Foeniculum vulgare*)
- Basil (*Ocimum basilicum*)
- Lime peel (*Citrus aurantiifolia*)
- Star anise (*Illicium verum*)

## Reported biological activity

### Human evidence
No verified controlled human study of inhaled isolated fenchol was identified for this archive [^2].

### Animal or laboratory evidence

{{include includes/preclinical-evidence-note.md}}

In vitro screens report TRPA1 antagonism and FFAR2 (GPR43) agonism at micromolar concentrations, plus antimicrobial and antioxidant activity in essential-oil or cell-free assays; none establish effects at cannabis-relevant inhaled exposure [^3].

### Traditional or anecdotal claims
Used in traditional preparations of fennel and basil for digestive and respiratory use.
