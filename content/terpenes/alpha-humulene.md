# α-Humulene

## Identity

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Preferred name | α-Humulene (α-Caryophyllene) |
| IUPAC name | (1E,4E,8E)-2,6,6,9-tetramethylcycloundeca-1,4,8-triene |
| CAS number | 6753-98-6 |
| PubChem CID | 5281520 [^3] |
| Chemical family | Monocyclic Sesquiterpene |
| Molecular formula | C15H24 |
| Molecular mass | 204.35 g/mol |
| Stereochemistry | No chiral centers; the (1E,4E,8E) double-bond geometry is fixed in the name. Constitutional isomer of β-caryophyllene (same formula, different skeleton), never collapsed with it |

## Aroma and sensory character

Earthy, woody, herbal, hoppy aroma profile.

## Occurrence outside cannabis

- Hops (*Humulus lupulus*)
- Coriander (*Coriandrum sativum*)
- Sage (*Salvia officinalis*)

## Reported biological activity

### Human evidence
Clinical evidence for isolated human oral or vaporized humulene administration is minimal.

### Animal or laboratory evidence

{{include includes/preclinical-evidence-note.md}}

In a murine model of allergic airway inflammation, α-humulene administered orally or by aerosol reduced eosinophil infiltration and markers such as IL-5 and LTB4 [^2].

### Traditional or anecdotal claims
Associated with traditional hoppy medicinal infusions.

## Cannabis laboratory results

Reported at roughly 0.1–2 mg/g dry weight in dried flower across cultivars (GC-MS of hydrodistilled essential oil and chemotype surveys) [^4]. Values are batch- and method-dependent. Refer to [Lab Results](../lab-results.md) for batch data.
