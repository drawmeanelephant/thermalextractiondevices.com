# Cultivar Name, Product Name, and Chemovar

## Taxonomy Matrix

| Level | Definition | Entity Scope | Factual Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Genetic Cultivar** | Botanical lineage and breeder history | Overview / Index | First-party breeder documentation |
| **Producer Product** | Commercial packaged offering sold by a licensed producer | Packaging / Brand | Licensee product registration |
| **Batch Chemovar** | Measured quantitative chemical composition of a specific lot | Factual Data Node | Laboratory Certificate of Analysis (COA) |

## Key Technical Distinctions

1. **Cultivar vs Chemovar**: A cultivar (e.g. *Blue Dream*) represents a genetic family, not a static chemical formula. The exact chemovar (terpene percentages and cannabinoid ratios) varies across environment, nutrition, harvest time, and curing methods.
2. **Indica/Sativa Labels**: Botanical morphology descriptors (*indica* broad-leaf vs *sativa* narrow-leaf) do not correlate reliably with chemical composition or pharmacological response.
3. **Data Integrity Standard**: In the Thermal Extraction Devices archive, batch COA quantitative measurements serve as the single factual source of record.
