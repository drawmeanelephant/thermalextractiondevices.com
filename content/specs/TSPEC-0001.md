# TSPEC-0001 Condenser Thermal Profile

Performance baseline for [TED-0001](../devices/TED-0001.md) dual-stage condenser assemblies.

## Mechanical Parameters

- **Weight**: 14.2 kg
- **Footprint**: 450mm x 320mm x 210mm
- **Fitting Interfaces**: 3/8" Tri-Clamp Sanitary Flange

## Thermodynamic Limits

- **Continuous Duty Flux**: 4.5 kW thermal dissipation
- **Peak Thermal Pulse Limit**: 6.2 kW for < 300s
