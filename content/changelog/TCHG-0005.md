# TCHG-0005 Screen Baller Reclassified as a Component

Corrects a misclassification introduced in TCHG-0004. Changelog records are
append-only, so TCHG-0004 stands as written and this record supersedes it on
this point.

- Retired `devices/TED-0040` (FlowerPot Screen Baller). Part 3408 is a diffuser
  insert, not a device model: its listing sold only the diffuser and a 3/4"
  titanium screen — no top or nut, nothing that forms a working head — and the
  same part is sold today as the "22mm Standard Diffuser", shipping inside the
  B1 and B2 heads. A device record double-counted a part already recorded as a
  spec-table row on the heads that contain it.
- **TED-0040 is retired and must never be reused.** Canonical IDs are immutable;
  a withdrawn record burns its number.
- TCHG-0004 had recorded part 3408 in "two roles across time", as both a model
  and a component. That framing is withdrawn. A part is classified once.
- Added rule **TAX-06** to the Device Architecture Taxonomy: a marketing name
  does not promote a component to a model. The discriminator is what a listing
  sold, not what it was called — a listing offering the parts needed to complete
  a working head is an assembly and may earn a record; a listing offering only an
  insert that drops into a head the buyer already owns is a component. TAX-06 is
  a review rule rather than a machine check, because deciding it requires reading
  the manufacturer's listing.
- Retained `devices/TED-0039` (Baller Head Assembly, part 3405), which passes the
  same test: its listing sold top-with-dish variants and could be bought as a
  complete head.
- Rewired the lineage: the B2 now `supersedes` the Baller directly, with the
  Screen Baller described in prose on both records as the interstitial step that
  holds no record of its own.
- Recorded the exclusion in the family lineage guide's "deliberately not modeled"
  table and in the manufacturer record, so the omission reads as a decision.
