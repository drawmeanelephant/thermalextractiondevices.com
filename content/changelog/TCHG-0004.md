# TCHG-0004 Cannabis Hardware Corpus Completion

Completed the Cannabis Hardware device corpus begun in TCHG-0003's sibling work,
taking the manufacturer from partial coverage to a fully classified catalog.

- Added ten device records: the current ZenLeaf diffuser heads VMAX (TED-0034),
  VMAX Injector (TED-0035), and Mercury (TED-0036); the pre-ball FlowerPot heads
  Showerhead (TED-0037) and Vrod (TED-0038); the first two ball-vape generations,
  the Baller (TED-0039) and the Screen Baller (TED-0040); the Zion base station
  (TED-0041); and the cordless 20 mm-coil heads Pulse (TED-0042) and Swift
  (TED-0043).
- Recovered the pre-ball lineage from archived NewVape and Cannabis Hardware
  listings, establishing NewVape-era part numbers and observed listing windows in
  place of unpublished release dates.
- Established that a deliberately unmodeled SKU is recorded with the rule that
  excludes it, so that omissions are decisions rather than gaps. A sweep of the
  manufacturer's full ZenLeaf and FlowerPot collections found no further
  substantive platform unmodeled.
- Corrected three attribution errors: the MOAB Essentials Kit bundles the Mercury
  (part 3518), not a VMAX (part 3574); the archived "B-2 Head Assembly" page
  carries the Baller's description, so neither its "Introduced in Aug-2021" line
  nor its ball figures are B-2 specifications; and the B1 head was listed from
  2021-10, with the 2022-02 date belonging to the kit.
- Recorded the ZenLeaf Rev 2 heat-deflector plate (2023-11-29) identically across
  all four second-generation station records.
- Corrected the second-generation ZenLeaf station status from Current to
  discontinued, distinguishing manufacturer-stated discontinuation (Nova, Fusion)
  from delisting evidence (Whisper, Bliss).
- Documented that Boris caps `relations` at sixteen per page, and that a
  manufacturer record therefore indexes its devices through prose and the family
  lineage guide rather than a reciprocal relation list.
- Cross-linked the Device Architecture Taxonomy (TREF-0004) and the Cannabis
  Hardware Family Lineage (TGDE-0006) as standard and reference application.
