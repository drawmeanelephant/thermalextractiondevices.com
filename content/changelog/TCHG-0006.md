# TCHG-0006 Record Completeness Floor

Established and enforced a single quality floor across the entire device corpus,
then raised every record to it.

- Measured the corpus against its own standard for the first time. The Device
  Architecture Taxonomy has described five orthogonal axes since TCHG-0004's
  predecessor, but **no record carried a tag on all five**: heat generation was
  absent from 44 of 45 records and power from 43. Part numbers appeared on 11.
  Two records cited no primary source at all. Depth ranged 3.9× between the
  thinnest and richest record.
- Added `scripts/audit_record_completeness.py` with rules REC-01 to REC-06 and
  thirteen unit tests, and wired it into `bin/validate_graph.sh` so the floor is
  enforced on every build rather than remembered.
- Raised all 44 device records to the floor: every record now carries a tag on
  each of the five axes, a part-number row, a primary-source citation, a safety
  section, and a component-role or form-factor row. The audit reports zero errors
  and zero warnings, down from 81 errors and 51 warnings.
- Closed the archive's only two evidence holes. TED-0021 (IOLITE Original) and
  TED-0022 (WISPR 2) previously rested entirely on internal research provenance
  with no citation a reader could follow; both now cite the manufacturer's
  operation manual and the Oglesby & Butler catalytic-vaporiser patent.
- Recorded that an absent part number is stated rather than omitted: "Not
  published by the manufacturer" satisfies REC-02, because the reader learns the
  identifier does not exist instead of wondering whether the archive forgot.
- Recorded that internal research provenance is a pointer, not a citation. A
  record may not rest on the research corpus alone.

The unevenness this corrects was structural rather than anyone's error: newer
records carried part numbers and component roles that older ones lacked, while
older records carried delivery-mode and operating-mode tags that newer ones
lacked. Nothing measured the difference, so it accumulated silently. The audit
now does.
