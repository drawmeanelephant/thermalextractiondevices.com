## Regulatory Overview

Maryland legalized adult use in 2022 (Question 4) with retail sales from July 1, 2023, regulated by the Maryland Cannabis Administration. The medical program (2014) continues. MCA publishes an interactive data dashboard; no machine-readable datasets were located on data.maryland.gov (verified 2026-08-08).
