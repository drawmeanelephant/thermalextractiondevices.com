## Regulatory Overview

Florida's medical marijuana program (Amendment 2, approved 2016) is administered by the Office of Medical Marijuana Use within the Department of Health. The Medical Marijuana Use Registry tracks qualified physicians, patients, and caregivers. Adult use is not legal; a 2024 ballot measure to legalize adult use failed. Home cultivation is not permitted.
