## Subnational Systems

## Regulatory Overview

Spain approved a Royal Decree (October 2025) regulating medical cannabis preparations for hospital care under specialist prescription, with an AEMPS public register of standardized preparations; implementation and first sales were expected through 2026. Private adult use is not criminalized in limited amounts, and cannabis social clubs operate in a tolerated grey zone without national legalization. Personal possession remains prohibited in public.
