## Regulatory Overview

Tennessee remains one of the states without a medical cannabis program; a narrow statutory provision allows possession of low-THC CBD oil (≤0.9% THC) with proof of a lawful order. Hemp-derived cannabinoid licensing rules took effect January 1, 2026, and a 2026 law restricts THC products at or above 0.3% concentration effective July 1, 2026.
