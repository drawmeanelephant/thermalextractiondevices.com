## Regulatory Overview

Arizona legalized medical cannabis in 2010 (Proposition 203) and adult use in 2020 (Proposition 207), with retail sales beginning January 2021. ADHS administers both programs. The department publishes monthly medical program statistics as PDF reports; no public batch/testing chemistry or open-data downloads were located (2026-08-08 verification).
