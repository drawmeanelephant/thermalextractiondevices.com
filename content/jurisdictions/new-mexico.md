## Regulatory Overview

New Mexico legalized adult use under the Cannabis Regulation Act (2021), with retail sales from April 2022, regulated by the Cannabis Control Division of the RLD. The medical program dates to 2007. The Division maintains a C.R.O.P. data catalog with downloadable datasets, though the dataset list did not render in a static fetch (contents unverified).
