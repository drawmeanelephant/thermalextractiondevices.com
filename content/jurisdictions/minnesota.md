## Regulatory Overview

Minnesota legalized adult use under HF 100 (2023), with possession and home cultivation effective August 1, 2023 and licensed retail rolling out through 2025–2026 under the Office of Cannabis Management. The medical cannabis program (2014) continues. Minnesota's licensing framework includes social equity provisions.
