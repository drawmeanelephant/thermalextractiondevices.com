## Regulatory Overview

A law effective January 1, 2026 legalized personal cannabis possession and home cultivation for adults 21 and older (up to 3 plants, 25 g in public / 100 g at home), building on prior decriminalization. Commercial sales remain prohibited. Medical cannabis has been regulated since 2013 under SUKL, and cannabis-based products are available by prescription.
