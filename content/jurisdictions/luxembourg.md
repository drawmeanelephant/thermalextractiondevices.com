## Regulatory Overview

Luxembourg's law of July 10, 2023 (amending the 1973 law) legalized personal possession (up to 3 g) and home cultivation (up to 4 plants per household from seed) for adults 18+, with public consumption prohibited. There is no legal commercial supply; a retail model remains under discussion. This places Luxembourg in the "personal cultivation" tier of European reforms alongside Germany and Malta.
