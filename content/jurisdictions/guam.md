## Regulatory Overview

Guam legalized adult-use cannabis under the Guam Cannabis Industry Act of 2019, administered by the Cannabis Control Board within the Department of Revenue and Taxation. After years of regulatory delay, the first facility permit was issued in June 2026 and the first adult-use cultivation license in July 2026; a legal market is still being stood up.
