## Regulatory Overview

The CNMI legalized adult use and personal cultivation under the CNMI Cannabis Act of 2018 (the first U.S. territory to do so). A commercial market has not become operational as of retrieval; the Cannabis Commission's regulatory implementation remains incomplete. Verify current status before any deep ingestion.
