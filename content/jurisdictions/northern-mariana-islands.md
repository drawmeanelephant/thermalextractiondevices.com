## Regulatory Overview

The CNMI legalized adult use and personal cultivation under the CNMI Cannabis Act of 2018 (the first U.S. territory to do so). Official Commonwealth Register records dated May 15, 2025 contain public notices for lifting the marijuana producer-license moratorium and certifying/adopting Cannabis Commission regulations. This pass located no current official sales or license registry evidence, so commercial operation remains unverified rather than affirmatively “stalled.” Verify current status before any deep ingestion.
