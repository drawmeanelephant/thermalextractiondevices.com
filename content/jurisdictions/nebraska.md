## Regulatory Overview

Nebraska voters approved two medical cannabis initiatives in November 2024. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission was established to implement the program; permanent regulations were approved by the governor in July 2026, with manufacturing license application periods opened in mid-2026. Adult use remains prohibited.
