## Regulatory Overview

Thailand decriminalized cannabis in June 2022 (removing it from the narcotics list), but a June 25, 2025 Ministry of Public Health order prohibited recreational sale and made retail require medical authorization, reversing toward a medical-only policy. By 2026, cannabis is treated as medical/health-use; thousands of business licences have expired or been slated for expiry in 2026–2027. Policy volatility is high — verify current status before any reuse.
