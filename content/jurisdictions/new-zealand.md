## Regulatory Overview

New Zealand's Medicinal Cannabis Scheme (Misuse of Drugs (Medicinal Cannabis) Amendment Act 2018; operational April 2020) is administered by a Medicinal Cannabis Agency within the Ministry of Health, with Medsafe assessing products against a minimum quality standard. A 2020 referendum on adult-use legalization failed. Licensed cultivation, manufacture, and import supply the market; no public per-batch COA database was located.
