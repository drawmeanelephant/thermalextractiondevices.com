## Regulatory Overview

D.C. voters approved Initiative 71 (2014), legalizing possession of up to two ounces and home cultivation (six plants, three mature) plus gifting among adults. Because Congress has historically barred the District from using local funds to implement a regulated adult-use market, there is no licensed adult-use retail; purchases occur through the medical program (DOH) and unlicensed gift markets that ABCA enforcement has targeted.
