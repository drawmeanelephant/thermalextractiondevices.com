## Regulatory Overview

Utah's medical cannabis program (Utah Medical Cannabis Act, enacted 2018 following Proposition 2) is administered by the Center for Medical Cannabis, which licenses medical cannabis pharmacies and processors and manages the Electronic Verification System (EVS) for patients and providers. Adult use remains prohibited; patients do not cultivate at home.
