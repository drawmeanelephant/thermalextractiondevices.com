## Regulatory Overview

Georgia's medical cannabis program, administered by the Georgia Access to Medical Cannabis Commission (GMCC), historically allowed registered patients to possess low-THC oil. The Putting Georgia's Patients First Act (SB 220, signed May 2026, effective July 1, 2026) renamed "low THC oil" to "medical cannabis," removed the prior THC-percentage cap, expanded qualifying conditions, raised possession allowances, and permitted vaporization by adults 21+. Adult use remains prohibited; home cultivation is not authorized.
