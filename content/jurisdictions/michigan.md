## Regulatory Overview

Michigan legalized adult use in 2018 (Proposal 1), with retail sales from December 2019, regulated by the Cannabis Regulatory Agency. The medical program dates to 2008. CRA publishes monthly reports as PDFs (sales, prices, license counts); no public per-batch chemistry was located.
