## Regulatory Overview

Illinois legalized adult use through the Cannabis Regulation and Tax Act (2019), with retail sales beginning January 1, 2020. The medical program dates to 2013. Illinois publishes program reports on the IDFPR/ILCC sites, but no cannabis datasets were located on data.illinois.gov (catalog query empty, verified 2026-08-08), giving the state a low machine-readability score.
