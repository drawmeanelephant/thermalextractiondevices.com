## Regulatory Overview

Indiana has no medical or adult-use cannabis program. State law permits low-THC hemp extract (≤0.3% THC and at least 5% CBD). House Bill 1285 (effective July 1, 2026) added regulation of THC-containing products, and legislative efforts to establish a medical program have not been enacted as of retrieval.
