## Regulatory Overview

The U.S. Virgin Islands legalized adult use under the Virgin Islands Cannabis Use Act (Act 8680, signed January 2023), administered by the Office of Cannabis Regulations, which manages medicinal, sacramental, and adult-use licensing. Business licensing opened in 2025 (manufacturing licenses first). Act 9072 (January 23, 2026) added rules for intoxicating hemp and synthetic cannabinoids.
