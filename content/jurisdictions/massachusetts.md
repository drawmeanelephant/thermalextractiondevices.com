## Regulatory Overview

Massachusetts legalized adult use under ballot Question 3 (2016), with retail sales beginning November 2018. The Cannabis Control Commission licenses cultivation, manufacturing, retail, delivery, and testing laboratories, and administers the medical program (2012). The CCC maintains an **open-data catalog** with license trackers, testing results (2024–2025), sales, price-per-gram, and plant-activity datasets, plus public health and safety advisories.
