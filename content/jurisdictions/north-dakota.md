## Regulatory Overview

North Dakota's medical marijuana program (Compassionate Care Act, Measure 5 of 2016) is administered by HHS, which registers patients and caregivers and licenses compassionate care centers. Program changes in 2025 added low-dose THC edibles and eligibility adjustments. Adult use remains prohibited.
