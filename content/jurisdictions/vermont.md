## Regulatory Overview

Vermont legalized adult-use possession in 2018 and commercial sales under Act 164 (2020), with the Cannabis Control Board licensing cultivation, manufacturing, wholesale, and retail. The medical program dates to 2004. The CCB adopted 2026 legislative changes (Acts 176 and 178); cultivation and retail application windows have been periodically closed to new applicants.
