## Regulatory Overview

Rhode Island legalized adult use under the Rhode Island Cannabis Act (2022), with sales from December 1, 2022, regulated by the Cannabis Control Commission. The medical program dates to 2006. Additional adult-use retail licenses opened for application in August 2026; 2026 legislative amendments (S 2961 and related) refined the licensing framework.
