## Regulatory Overview

Colombia legalized medical cannabis under Law 1787 (2016) with a three-authority licensing model: ICA (cultivation), INVIMA (manufacture/sanitary registration), and the FNE/Ministry of Justice (psychoactive-use licenses). Personal-dose amounts (≈20 g) are not criminalized under court jurisprudence. In 2026, new rules advanced for dry-flower medical dispensaries and renewed legislative pushes toward broader legalization were introduced but not enacted.
