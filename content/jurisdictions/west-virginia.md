## Regulatory Overview

West Virginia's medical cannabis program (Senate Bill 386, 2017) is administered by the Office of Medical Cannabis, which registers patients and certifying physicians and licenses growers, processors, and dispensaries. Adult use remains prohibited; home cultivation is not permitted.
