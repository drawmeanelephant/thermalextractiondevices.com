## Regulatory Overview

Delaware legalized adult-use possession in 2023 (HB 1/HB 2) and launched adult-use retail sales on August 1, 2025, regulated by the Office of the Marijuana Commissioner. Home cultivation and public consumption remain prohibited. The medical marijuana program (established 2011) continues under DHSS.
