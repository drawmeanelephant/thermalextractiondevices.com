## Regulatory Overview

Arkansas voters approved the Arkansas Medical Marijuana Amendment (2016), establishing a patient registry administered by the Department of Health and dispensary/grower licensing administered by the Medical Marijuana Commission and Alcoholic Beverage Control. The program serves qualifying patients; adult use and home cultivation are not authorized.
