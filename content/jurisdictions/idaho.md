## Regulatory Overview

Idaho has no regulated cannabis program; the use, possession, and sale of cannabis for any purpose remain illegal under state law. A medical cannabis ballot initiative for 2026 did not qualify for the ballot. The Office of Drug Policy publishes the state's marijuana policy position.
