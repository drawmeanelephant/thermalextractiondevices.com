## Regulatory Overview

Wyoming has no regulated medical or adult-use cannabis program; cannabis remains illegal. Hemp plants at or below 0.3% THC are legal, but 2024 legislation criminalized hemp products containing psychoactive substances (upheld against a Farm Bill preemption challenge in the Tenth Circuit, October 2025). 2026 legislation (HB 0166) addressing marijuana scheduling was not enacted as of retrieval.
