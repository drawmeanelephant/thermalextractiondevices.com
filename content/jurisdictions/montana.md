## Regulatory Overview

Montana legalized adult use under Initiative 190 (2020), with retail sales beginning January 2022, regulated by the Department of Revenue's Cannabis Control Division. The medical program dates to 2004. The Department publishes cannabis sales reports, product recall pages, and license information.
