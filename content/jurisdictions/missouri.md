## Regulatory Overview

Missouri legalized medical cannabis in 2018 and adult use in 2022 (Amendment 3), with adult-use retail from February 2023, regulated by the DHSS Division of Cannabis Regulation. The state publishes a sales dashboard (data from October 2020 forward); no documented API was located, and the seed-to-sale contract was in flux during 2026 (source-stability risk).
