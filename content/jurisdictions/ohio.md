## Regulatory Overview

Ohio legalized adult use under Issue 2 (November 2023), with home grow effective December 7, 2023 and licensed retail from August 2024, regulated by the Division of Cannabis Control. The medical program dates to 2016. A 2026 law (SB 56) reined in possession limits and restricted intoxicating hemp products. License lookup uses a Salesforce-based search; no open-data downloads were located.
