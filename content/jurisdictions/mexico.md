## Cannabis Framework

| Dimension | Status |
| --- | --- |

| Adult-use cultivation | Not legalized generally (SCJN rulings authorize self-consumption for named individuals; no general program) |
| Adult-use commercial supply | Not legal (no regulated commercial adult-use market) |
| Medical access | Regulated medical cannabis (2017 General Health Law reform; 2021 regulations) under COFEPRIS |
| Pharmaceutical cannabinoid access | Registered cannabis-derived medicines (≤1% THC products and others per regulation) |
| Home cultivation | Not legalized generally |
| Import/export | Regulated; industrial use authorization per 2021 court decision |

## Regulatory Overview

Mexico decriminalized personal adult cannabis self-consumption through Supreme Court decisions culminating in a June 2021 general declaration of unconstitutionality of the absolute prohibition, but the legislature has not enacted a regulated market. Medical cannabis has been regulated since 2017 (General Health Law reform; regulations in 2021) under COFEPRIS, which in July 2026 clarified its position on sanitary authorizations for personal adult use. Policy remains in flux.
