## Regulatory Overview

Wisconsin has no medical or adult-use cannabis program; possession remains illegal under state law, though a number of municipalities have local decriminalization ordinances. Medical cannabis bills were introduced in 2026 (e.g., SB 1045) but not enacted as of retrieval. The 2026 federal Schedule III rescheduling does not change Wisconsin's state-law prohibition.
