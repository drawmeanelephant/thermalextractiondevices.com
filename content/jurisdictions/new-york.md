## Regulatory Overview

New York legalized adult use under the Marijuana Regulation and Taxation Act (2021), with the Office of Cannabis Management regulating adult-use, medical, and cannabinoid-hemp programs. The license registry on data.ny.gov is among the cleanest of the large markets, with full entity/tier/cultivation fields via SODA. No public per-batch chemistry is published.

## Regulator History

- **2014**: Compassionate Care Act establishes the medical program.
- **2021**: Marijuana Regulation and Taxation Act establishes OCM and legalizes adult use.
- **2022**: First adult-use dispensaries open (December 2022).
- **Present**: Market scaling; license registry published on data.ny.gov.

## Licensing Categories

Cultivator (indoor/outdoor/mixed-light tiers), processor, distributor, retail dispensary, on-site consumption, delivery, microbusiness, cooperative, and registered organizations (medical). Registered Retail Dealers dataset complements the main registry.

## Testing Laboratory Framework

- **Contaminant limits**: Part 125 panels.
- **Lab registry**: No public lab registry dataset found.
- **Public results**: None — chemistry is not published.
