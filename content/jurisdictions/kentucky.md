## Regulatory Overview

Kentucky legalized medical cannabis under Senate Bill 47 (2023), with the program effective January 1, 2025, administered by the Office of Medical Cannabis. Since July 1, 2026, cardholders are expected to purchase only from licensed Kentucky dispensaries. A June 2026 executive order clarified qualifying conditions. Adult use remains prohibited.
