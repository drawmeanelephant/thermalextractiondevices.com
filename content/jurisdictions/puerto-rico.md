## Regulatory Overview

Puerto Rico established its medical cannabis program under Act 42-2017, administered by the Department of Health, with licensed dispensaries serving registered patients. Adult-use legislation has not been enacted as of retrieval. Regulator identity and program details should be re-verified against current official materials before deep ingestion.
