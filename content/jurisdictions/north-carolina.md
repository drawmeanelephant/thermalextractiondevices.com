## Regulatory Overview

North Carolina has no state medical or adult-use cannabis program. Regulated cannabis sales occur only on tribal trust land operated by the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians (Great Smoky Cannabis Co.). The state has been restricting intoxicating hemp-derived products (2026 legislation) and a state medical program has not been enacted.
