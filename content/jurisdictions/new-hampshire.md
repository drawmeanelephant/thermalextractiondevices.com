## Regulatory Overview

New Hampshire's Therapeutic Cannabis Program (2013) is administered by DHHS, which registers qualifying patients, caregivers, and certifying providers and licenses alternative treatment centers. Adult-use legislation has not been enacted; the state remains medical-only with no home-cultivation allowance.
