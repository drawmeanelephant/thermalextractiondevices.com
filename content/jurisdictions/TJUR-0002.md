# Massachusetts

The **Massachusetts Cannabis Control Commission** (CCC) is the state agency regulating cannabis in Massachusetts. This archive compiles the Commission's official open data, public health and safety advisories, and published industry reporting.

## Public Health and Safety Advisories

The Commission publishes **public health and safety advisories** for contaminated or potentially contaminated products. These are modeled under [[safety-advisories]] and are not relabeled as recalls unless the Commission itself uses that term.
