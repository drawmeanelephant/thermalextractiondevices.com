## Regulatory Overview

Kansas has no regulated medical or adult-use cannabis program; cannabis remains illegal for all purposes. Medical cannabis bills introduced in 2025–2026 (e.g., the Kansas Health Freedom Act) were not enacted as of retrieval. The state adopted a 2026 law (HB 2365, effective July 1, 2026) affecting certain THC-containing products and kratom.
