## Regulatory Overview

Pennsylvania's Medical Marijuana Program (Act 16 of 2016) is administered by the Department of Health, which licenses growers/processors, dispensaries, and clinical registrants and registers patients/caregivers. Adult-use legislation has not been enacted; home cultivation is not permitted.
