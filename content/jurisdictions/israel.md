## Regulatory Overview

Israel operates one of the world's largest medical cannabis programs, administered by the Ministry of Health's Medical Cannabis Unit (IMCA) under Procedure 106. The 2019–2021 reforms moved patients to licensed producers/pharmacies and expanded qualifying access. First-time small-quantity possession is decriminalized under police enforcement guidelines; adult-use legalization has not been enacted. In 2026, a health committee recommended phasing out smoked medical cannabis over three years.
