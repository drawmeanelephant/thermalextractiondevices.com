## Regulatory Overview

South Dakota voters approved both medical (IM 26) and adult-use (IM 27) measures in 2020; the adult-use measure was overturned in court, while the medical program was implemented and is administered by the Department of Health. The program uses METRC for seed-to-sale tracking. 2026 legislative attempts to repeal or further restrict the program were rejected.
