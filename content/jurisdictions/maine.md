## Regulatory Overview

Maine legalized adult use by referendum (2016) with retail sales from October 2020, regulated by the Office of Cannabis Policy. The medical program dates to 1999 and is administered separately. Maine publishes aggregate testing summaries and compliance data but no per-batch public results; the open-data surface is dashboard/report oriented with no documented API.
