## Regulatory Overview

Mississippi established its medical cannabis program under SB 2095 (2022, following the voter-approved Initiative 65 framework), administered by the Mississippi Department of Health Medical Cannabis Program. The program licenses cultivation, processing, testing, and dispensing. Adult use remains prohibited; home cultivation is not permitted.
