## Revision Notes

- The 2.0 is a major architectural redesign of the 2021 refresh: replaceable pan, stainless steel vapor path with intercooler, adjustable airport, thermal block, and lower weight (~17 g vs ~25 g) [^1].
- Available as **Pure** (no mica heat shield; carbonized wood interior) and **Classic** (mica silicate heat shield, oiled finish) variants [^1].
- Superseded by the [Vapman Click](../devices/TED-0012.md) (late 2024), which added the bi-metal click temperature indicator and a larger bowl [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer guidance: use only jet-flame lighters; no solvents on wood; do not overheat the pan (gold flake risk) [^1][^3].
