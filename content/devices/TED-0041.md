## Family Context

- Zion is positioned by the manufacturer as "the smallest and most affordable device in the ZenLeaf vaporizer series," listed from 2024-03-28 [^1]. **Cross-reference note:** the manufacturer's earlier ZenLeaf blog (2023-09-era base-station launch) makes the analogous claim for the Whisper — "the smallest footprint in the ZenLeaf series of cordless Dry Herb Vaporizers" [^2]. Both are manufacturer claims about different products at different dates; this record documents both rather than resolving which is "smaller" by inference. The [Whisper](../devices/TED-0029.md) page is owned by a different contributor on this pass; a matching cross-reference note there is recommended.
- Zion shares "the same shielding concept as Moab, but without an integrated PID" [^1] — it is the BYO-PID counterpart to the [MOAB](../devices/TED-0032.md) within the current ZenLeaf lineup, and architecturally closest to the [Whisper](../devices/TED-0029.md) (also BYO-PID). It is distinguished from both by dual coil compatibility (20 mm or 25 mm), where Whisper and MOAB are 25 mm axial only [^1][^3][^4].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer safety instruction: DO NOT leave the device unattended while on/hot [^1].
- All accessories, including flower diffusers and bangers, are sold separately; the coil itself is also sold separately [^1].
