## Family Context

- Manufacturer description (Screen Baller (3408) product page, 2021-09-27 snapshot): "This iteration of the baller diffuser will allow for 3mm pearls. The original baller was designed and tested with 4mm pearls in mind, but our community loves to experiment, which revealed that 3mm pearls would obstruct the holes. This part addresses that issue, and is able to take on any size pearl. Grade 2 Titanium Screen allows for any size pearls/balls to be used. Includes: - Screen Baller Diffuser (3408) - 3/4" Titanium Screen (3103)." The same listing repeats the Generation 1 "About the Baller" boilerplate describing the hollowed-Vrod origin [^1].
- Manufacturer evolution blog (2021-10-20): "We didn't think it could get much better than the Flowerpot Baller but we were wrong. Next came the Screen Baller. This diffuser kept the same shape as the original baller diffuser but the machined bottom was replaced with a screen. This allowed for the use of smaller sized balls and made for a more even heating of the shovelhead bowl." [^2]
- **The "Screen Baller" branding was retired, but SKU 3408 was not discontinued.** The exact same SKU was later re-listed as "22mm Baller Diffuser (3408)" (archived 2021-12-28 through 2022, description updated to reference the B2: "About the B2: The B2 is the replacement for our beloved Vrod head...") [^3], and today (live-checked 2026-08-08) is sold as "**22mm 'Standard' Diffuser (3408)**" at $71.00, "Rev C machined to accept both mesh and machined screens, started shipping 7/9/23," explicitly marketed as compatible with the current [B1](../devices/TED-0004.md) and B2 heads. Customer reviews on the live listing describe swapping it in to replace "the Vrod bottom" and "the old Vrod" — direct confirmation of the Vrod → Baller → Screen Baller → current-diffuser continuity from real buyers, cited here as **community-reported**, not manufacturer fact [^4][^7].
- The live `cannabishardware.com/products/screen-baller-3408` URL now 301-redirects to `cannabishardware.com/products/22mm-vaporizer-diffuser`, confirming the manufacturer itself treats the current listing as the same product's continuation rather than an unrelated replacement (checked 2026-08-08) [^4].
- Per the manufacturer's own lineage narrative, the Screen Baller was superseded by the B2 (its "own ecosystem" with a custom dish nut) — that record is [devices/TED-0028](../devices/TED-0028.md), owned by another worker in this pass. This record adds a `relates_to` link to TED-0028; the reciprocal `supersedes=devices/TED-0040` edge on the B2 record does not yet exist and is flagged to the integrator in the worker report.
