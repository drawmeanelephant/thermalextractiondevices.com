## Family Context

- Manufacturer description (Baller Head Assembly product page, 2021-09-27 snapshot): "Introduced in Aug-2021. The Baller is the newest head designed for the flowerpot ecosystem. We started with the legendary Vrod head and machined away the inside to accommodate 58 Quartz 4 mm spheres. This design more than doubles the heater's surface area. Instead of the straight air channels found in the Vrod, the balls create an obstructed air path forcing the air around the balls resulting in better efficiency. You are able to customize your experience by using different material balls that are widely available. (Ruby, Sapphire, Quartz, SiC)." [^1]
- **Variant structure (2021-09-27 snapshot), quoted verbatim:** "NO QUARTZ BALLS / NO TOP - $66.00 USD", "NO QUARTZ BALLS / TOP WITH SIC DISH (+$80) - $146.00 USD", "NO QUARTZ BALLS / TOP WITH SAPPHIRE DISH (+205) - $271.00 USD", "ADD QUARTZ BALLS (+$18) / NO TOP - Sold Out", "ADD QUARTZ BALLS (+$18) / TOP WITH SIC DISH (+$80) - Sold Out", "ADD QUARTZ BALLS (+$18) / TOP WITH SAPPHIRE DISH (+205) - Sold Out." The balls-included variants were sold out at the time of this snapshot; only the ball-less base and dish-topped variants were purchasable [^1].
- Manufacturer release blog (August 31, 2021): "If you have been following Troy @ 420 VapeZone then you have probably seen the 'B-Rod modification'... Here at Cannabis Hardware we have taken the idea of the ball modification to a whole other level with the latest custom diffuser called the Flowerpot 'Baller.' We have bored out the inside of the diffuser to add more room for pearls, rubies, or balls... The baller head comes with a screen to hold the balls inside of the head. We recommend using the baller with 4mm spherical terp pearls... The baller can only be used with the Vrod top, not the Weedeater." [^3]
- **Naming:** the manufacturer's product-page title was "Baller Head Assembly"; the family is retroactively labelled "FlowerPot Ball Vape" (Generation 1) in the already-published [manufacturer record](../manufacturers/TMFR-0004.md)'s family table. This archive record uses the manufacturer's own contemporaneous product title in the page title, and cross-references the retroactive family label [^5].
- Superseded within about three months by the [Screen Baller](../devices/TED-0040.md), which replaced this head's machined solid bottom with a screen bottom to accept smaller (3 mm) balls [^2].

## Safety Notes

- No head-specific safety warnings were found in the archived listing. General FlowerPot guidance applies by manufacturer practice at the family level: the heated head and any installed concentrate dish are burn hazards. This is carried over from later FlowerPot pages, not confirmed on this product's own listing — labelled here as **community/family-level guidance**.
