## Family Context

- VMAX Injector is the current-generation injector (male) cordless diffuser head in the ZenLeaf lineup, occupying the role Jane ([ZenLeaf Jane, TED-0027](../devices/TED-0027.md)) held in the first generation. The manufacturer does not publish a generational-succession statement for the VMAX Injector the way it does for the Mercury ("second generation Female Flower Diffuser" — see [TED-0036](../devices/TED-0036.md)); this record treats VMAX Injector↔Jane as a documented family lineage (`relates_to`), not a manufacturer-asserted `supersedes` [^1][^3].
- **No manufacturer-documented base-station bundle was found for this SKU.** The two ZenLeaf-adjacent Essentials kits checked — the MOAB "X Pattern" Essentials Kit (8065) and the Airstream Essentials Cordless Ball Vape Kit (8072) — were both fetched and searched; neither mentions part 3580 or "VMAX Injector." The Airstream kit bundles the standard [VMAX (3574)](../devices/TED-0044.md) instead. This record therefore does **not** assert a `relates_to` relation to [MOAB (TED-0032)](../devices/TED-0032.md) or [Airstream (TED-0033)](../devices/TED-0033.md); the injector shares the family's 25 mm axial-coil architecture (per the shared ZenLeaf collection) but per-model base-station compatibility is not separately manufacturer-published [^4][^5].
- Sibling head, same platform: the standard [VMAX](../devices/TED-0044.md) is the non-injector (female, 22 mm) counterpart [^1].
- A French retailer's mirrored listing (lacentralevapeur.com, reference "CH_3580") independently republishes the SKU, the convection-focused framing ("almost entirely convection-based"), and the included-components list, corroborating [^1] from a second source. This is a secondary retailer source, cited only for corroboration, not as primary evidence [^6].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer ZenLeaf warnings apply: the base and heated heads are burn hazards; do not leave the station or a hot head unattended [^3][^2].
