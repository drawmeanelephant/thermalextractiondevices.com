## Family Context

- VMAX is the current-generation standard/female (22 mm) cordless diffuser head in the ZenLeaf lineup, occupying the role Mary ([ZenLeaf Mary, TED-0026](../devices/TED-0026.md)) held in the first generation. The manufacturer does not publish a generational-succession statement for VMAX the way it does for the Mercury ("second generation Female Flower Diffuser" — see [TED-0036](../devices/TED-0036.md)); this record treats VMAX↔Mary as a documented family lineage (`relates_to`), not a manufacturer-asserted `supersedes` [^1][^2].
- VMAX is bundled with the [Airstream](../devices/TED-0033.md) base station under the correct part number: the Airstream Essentials Cordless Ball Vape Kit (8072) lists "VMAX Ball Vape Head (3574)" alongside the "Airstream Desktop Base Station (3556)," and carries a "Rev 3/5/26" note stating these kits "started shipping with the VMax Head" [^4].
- **MOAB pairing is NOT confirmed for this SKU.** The MOAB "X Pattern" Essentials Ball Vape Kit (8065) product page describes pairing the MOAB with "the Mercury Flower Diffuser" in prose, but its "Bundle Includes" list mislabels part **3518** — the Mercury ([TED-0036](../devices/TED-0036.md); listed 2024-04-02, eighteen months before VMAX was listed 2025-09-23) — as "VMAX Cordless Ball Vape with 3mm Rubies and Cocobolo handle (3518)." This is a dated manufacturer copy error, not evidence that VMAX (3574) is the MOAB kit's bundled head; this record deliberately omits a `relates_to=devices/TED-0032` relation on that basis. See [TED-0036](../devices/TED-0036.md) Family Context for the full resolution and see this worker's report for a flagged correction request against [TED-0032](../devices/TED-0032.md) [^5].
- Sibling head, same platform: the [VMAX Injector](../devices/TED-0045.md) is the injector-style (male) counterpart [^1].
- A French retailer's mirrored listing (lacentralevapeur.com, reference "CH_3574") independently republishes the SKU, the 355-ruby capacity, the 37-hole quartz screens, and the heat-soaking/conduction language, corroborating [^1] from a second source as of the page's last-crawled state. This is a secondary retailer source, cited only for corroboration, not as primary evidence [^6].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer ZenLeaf warnings apply: the base and heated heads are burn hazards; do not leave the station or a hot head unattended [^2][^3].
