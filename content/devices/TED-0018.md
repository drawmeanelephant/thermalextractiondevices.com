## Revision Notes

- The Dash+ supersedes the G Pen Dash (2020): hybrid heating (vs conduction), titanium chamber, spiral ceramic air path, larger battery, USB-C, and full-color LCD with haptics (reported) [^1].
- The G Pen Dash II (2024/25) later returned to a ceramic chamber with an OLED display and a ~30 s heat-up (reported) [^1].
