## Revision & Compatibility Notes

- The B0 was introduced as the budget entry point in the third-generation FlowerPot lineup, positioned below the B1 and B2 [^1][^2].
- The manufacturer credits the "B-Zero" concept to community input; the head is a single machined piece with dual screen grooves [^2].

## Safety Notes

- Heated head is a burn hazard; manufacturer pages warn against leaving the device unattended while hot [^4].
- Use the included spring clip and injector bowl per manufacturer instructions; verify coil seating before first use [^2].
