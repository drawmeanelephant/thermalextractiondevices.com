## Family Context

- The manufacturer explicitly calls the Mercury "Cannabis Hardware's second generation Female Flower Diffuser," directly superseding [Mary (TED-0026)](../devices/TED-0026.md) — a genuine, manufacturer-supported generational claim, unlike the VMAX/VMAX Injector lineage relative to Mary/Jane (see [TED-0034](../devices/TED-0034.md), [TED-0035](../devices/TED-0035.md)) [^1].
- The manufacturer states the Mercury's 3/4" air path holds "up to 240 x 3mm Rubies — twice the capacity of the Mary Diffuser." This is manufacturer-published evidence about Mary's ruby capacity; it is recorded here as a cross-reference for the integrator/Mary-page owner, since Mary's own record ([TED-0026](../devices/TED-0026.md)) currently cites ruby size only ("3 mm or 4 mm ruby balls per research dossier") without a quantity — this Mercury-page statement implies Mary holds roughly 120 rubies, but that number is not independently manufacturer-published and should not be added to TED-0026 without a primary source stating it directly [^1].
- Documented compatible with the [MOAB](../devices/TED-0032.md), the [Airstream](../devices/TED-0033.md), and "any system supporting a 25mm coil," per the manufacturer's own product-page wording [^1].
- **3518 part-number collision, resolved.** The MOAB "X Pattern" Essentials Ball Vape Kit (8065) product page correctly describes pairing the MOAB with "the Mercury Flower Diffuser" in its prose, but its "Bundle Includes" list mislabels the bundled part as "VMAX Cordless Ball Vape with 3mm Rubies and Cocobolo handle (3518)." Part 3518 is the Mercury (this record), listed by the manufacturer on 2024-04-02 — eighteen months before the VMAX (3574) was listed on 2025-09-23. This is a dated manufacturer copy inconsistency: the kit page's own prose and SKU disagree with its own bullet-list product name. The real MOAB-kit head is the Mercury, not a VMAX. Both URLs: https://www.cannabishardware.com/products/mercury-ball-vape (primary source establishing 3518 = Mercury) and https://www.cannabishardware.com/products/moab-ball-vape-kit (source of the mislabeling) [^1][^4].
- Sibling current-generation ZenLeaf heads: [VMAX](../devices/TED-0034.md) (standard/female, 22 mm) and [VMAX Injector](../devices/TED-0035.md) (injector/male) — the Mercury's 3/4" air path is a distinct, larger-bore interface from the VMAX platform's 22 mm interface [^1][^2].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer ZenLeaf warnings apply: the base and heated heads are burn hazards; do not leave the station or a hot head unattended [^1][^3].
