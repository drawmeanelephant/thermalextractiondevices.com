## Revision Notes

- The WISPR 2 supersedes the WISPR (2011): higher fixed temperature (210 °C vs 190 °C), ~30 s heat-up (vs ~30–40 s), larger oven, fold-away silicone mouthpiece, and a mesh vent grill (reported) [^1].
- The WISPR 2 appears to be the final butane model in the IOLITE line; the WISPR E (2024/25) shifted to a battery-powered design (reported) [^1].
- **Unresolved conflict, flagged rather than silently resolved:** Oglesby & Butler's own web-development agency states the IOLITE brand "ceased production in 2023" [^6], which predates the WISPR E dating above (2024/25, from internal dossier provenance). Neither claim is deleted; a reader should treat both as reported/uncertain until a primary source settles it.

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer/manual warnings: flammable gas under pressure, upright filling, do not overfill, wait 5 minutes if overfilled [^2].
