## Revision Notes

- The WISPR 2 supersedes the WISPR (2011): higher fixed temperature (210 °C vs 190 °C), ~30 s heat-up (vs ~30–40 s), larger oven, fold-away silicone mouthpiece, and a mesh vent grill [^1].
- The WISPR 2 appears to be the final butane model in the IOLITE line; the WISPR E (2024/25) shifted to a battery-powered design [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer/manual warnings: flammable gas under pressure, upright filling, do not overfill, wait 5 minutes if overfilled [^2].
