## Revision Notes

- The NXT is the manufacturer's re-engineered successor to the E-Nano OG: reworked thermals (runs cooler), weighted stable base, and a black plastic controller; the core diameter is unchanged [^1].
- The E-Nano XL (parallel 2024–2025 release) enlarges the heater core and bowl by ~50 % and uses wider stems that are **not** cross-compatible with NXT stems [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer guidance: do not apply alcohol or citrus cleaners to the wood body; the unit is designed for 24/7 operation at low dial settings; keep the serial number (on the box and unit base) for warranty claims [^2][^4].
