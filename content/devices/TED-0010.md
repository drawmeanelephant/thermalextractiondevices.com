## Revision Notes

- The NXT is the manufacturer's re-engineered successor to the E-Nano OG: reworked thermals (runs cooler), weighted stable base, and a black plastic controller; the core diameter is unchanged [^6].
- The E-Nano XL (parallel 2024–2025 release) enlarges the heater core and bowl by ≈50 % and uses wider stems that are **not** cross-compatible with NXT stems (XL existence per the official XL collection [^5]; ≈50 %/non-compatibility details not published there — uncertain) [^5][^6].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer guidance (E-Nano Quick Start Guide, applies across the family): never use alcohol- or citrus-based cleaners on the wood body, as they damage the natural shellac finish; the unit is designed to be run 24/7; never leave a glass stem resting on a hot heater port; black spots on the stem indicate combustion from too high a dial setting or too slow a draw [^7].
- The warranty is non-transferable and unit-specific [^4]; the printed location of the required identifying/serial number is not independently confirmed on a currently reachable EpicVape page [^6].
