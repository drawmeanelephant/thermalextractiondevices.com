## Revision Notes

- The Click is the fourth Vapman generation and the first to add active (acoustic) temperature feedback via a passive bi-metal disc — a fundamental change from the manual flame-timing of generations 1–3 [^1].
- Compared with the 2.0: larger bowl, intercooler preinstalled, and a reduced accessory bundle (semi-hardshell case only — no torch, funnel, or concentrate pad) [^1].
- The click disc is not user-serviceable; the heating pan is user-replaceable with supplied screws [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer guidance: use only jet-flame lighters; keep the torch below 40 °C operating spec; no solvents on wood; do not overheat the pan (gold flake risk); keep away from children [^1][^3].
- Heating technique is "heat past the click by a short margin"; exact timing is user-dependent [^2].
