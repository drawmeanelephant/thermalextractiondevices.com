## Revision Notes

- **Gen 1 (Muad-Dib)**: fixed screen, no brass trench insert (early); later runs added a user-replaceable clamp-on screen and brass insert [^1].
- **Gen 2 (MD Beta / MD "Doc" / MD Noir)**: replaceable screen standard, brass insert standard, magnification-lens lid standard; stainless exterior compatible with the Power Up [^1].
- The MD Dab Box reuses the Launch Box chassis concept for concentrates; the Launch Box screen remains non-user-replaceable while the MD screen is user-replaceable [^1][^2].

## Safety & Use Notes

- The ~900 °F operating temperature requires the included silicone drawing whip for vapor cooling [^1][^2].
- The screen is fragile; avoid metal tools and perform burn-off cleaning per the Flight Guide [^2].
