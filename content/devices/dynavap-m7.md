# DynaVap M7 (TED-0002)

## Technical Specifications

| Property | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Heating Method | Conduction-Dominant Thermal Mass Storage with Convection Draw |
| Operation Mode | On-Demand |
| Temperature Control | Bimetallic tactile acoustic indicator cap (~190–210 °C click threshold) |
| Bowl Capacity | ~0.1 g (Adjust-a-Bowl feature reduces to ~0.05 g) |
| Airpath Materials | Medical-grade 316 stainless steel stem, tip, and condenser |
| Power Source | Direct thermal input (Butane flame torch or electromagnetic induction heater) |
| Heat-Up Time | ~5–10 seconds (induction) / ~10–15 seconds (flame) |
| Compatible Media | Dry botanical flower; concentrates via DynAdapter coil |

## Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol

1. **Disassembly**: Remove Captive Cap, tip, condenser, and fluoroelastomer o-rings.
2. **Cleaning**: Soak stainless steel stem, tip, and condenser in isopropyl alcohol. (Do not soak Captive Cap in alcohol prolonged; warm soapy water rinse recommended for cap).
3. **Reassembly**: Lubricate o-rings with DynAWax or hemp oil and reassemble condenser into stem body.
