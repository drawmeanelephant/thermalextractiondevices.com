## Revision Notes

- **TinyMight 1.5** (c. 2020–2021): a community-designated transitional run with tighter tolerances and improved button/battery door. The manufacturer never used the "1.5" designation; the distinction is identifiable only by build-date/serial ranges [^1].
- The OG was superseded by the TinyMight 2 (2022), which changed the heater to a 70 W stainless steel spiral, added USB-C, and enlarged the body [^1][^2].
- Early units carry no serial number; "blue PCB = fake" is a community anti-counterfeit heuristic, not a manufacturer statement [^1].
