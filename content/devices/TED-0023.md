## Revision Notes

- **Cloud+ (Gen 1.5, ≈2013)**: interim revision moving the air-flow restriction from the top of the Bamboo (near the joint) to the bottom intake; otherwise identical housing and heater; backwards compatible with Gen 1 accessories [^1].
- Superseded by the [Cloud EVO](../devices/TED-0024.md) (May 2013) [^1].
- Replacement parts for the Cloud were reported as unavailable by 2016 [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- AC mains device with a fixed (non-removable) power cord on early units; 110 V NA and 220 V EU units are not interchangeable — do not use a voltage converter [^1]. No Gen-1-specific manufacturer safety documentation is otherwise known to survive the company's closure; this note is derived from the record's own already-cited power specification rather than a dedicated manufacturer safety page.
