## Revision & Compatibility Notes

- The F22 (diffuser) and F16 (injector) launched together in June 2023 as the fourth-generation FlowerPot heads [^1].
- The F22 is the diffuser ("standard"/female) counterpart to the F16 injector: it sits on top of the bowl rather than injecting into it, covering more bowl surface area [^1].
- As of 2026-08-08 the F22 has no dedicated manufacturer product page (unlike the F16); its lineage and current status come from the third-party launch review and the manufacturer's FlowerPot collection page. Spec rows not covered by the manufacturer FAQ or collection page are attributed to the review.

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer FlowerPot safety guidance applies: do not leave the heated head unattended while hot; the heated assembly is a burn hazard until fully cooled [^5].
- The ruby balls must be seated per manufacturer instructions; verify coil seating before first use [^1].
