## Revision & Compatibility Notes

- The F22 (diffuser) and F16 (injector) launched together in June 2023 as the fourth-generation FlowerPot heads, positioned as a mid-tier value between the B-Zero and the B1/B2 [^1].
- The F22 is the diffuser ("standard"/female) counterpart to the F16 injector: it seats and seals over the outer edges of the Shovelhead bowl rather than injecting into it, covering more bowl surface area [^1].
- As of 2026-08-08 the F22 has no dedicated manufacturer product page (unlike the F16). Spec rows not covered by the manufacturer FAQ or collection page are credited to Troy's 420VapeZone review, "New Flowerpot Ball Vape Heads: F16 and F22 vs B-Zero, B1 and B2" (June 2023): he found the F22 delivers a hit similar to the B2 in impact and flavor "but with better flavor, more airflow, and less money invested" [^1].

## Safety Notes

- Troy (420VapeZone) documented that the F22/F16 floating screen is loose-fitting and held in place only by the ball fill; if the head is underfilled to any degree, the screen can wiggle loose and release balls. Keep the head tightly packed with balls before use [^1].
- Manufacturer FlowerPot safety guidance applies: do not leave the heated head unattended while hot; the heated assembly is a burn hazard until fully cooled [^6].
