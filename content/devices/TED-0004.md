## Revision Notes

- **Rev D (2023-09-01)**: integrated machined screen replaced the previous screen assembly (per manufacturer product history) [^1].
- The B1 shares the third-generation FlowerPot lineage with the [B2](../devices/TED-0028.md); the B2 (discontinued; no date manufacturer-published) was the dual-use variant with a concentrate dish [^1][^4].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer product pages warn against leaving a hot head unattended; the heated assembly is a burn hazard until fully cooled [^5].
- Do not exceed manufacturer-recommended operating temperatures; overheating can damage the head assembly [^5].
