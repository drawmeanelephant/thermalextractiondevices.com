## Lineage Notes

- The E-Nano OG was the first log-form-factor device from EpicVape, following the whip-style EV-2 [^1].
- The EV family (EV-1 beta c. 2010–2011; EV-2 ~May 2012) used a whip interface with an analog dial and Boost button; the E-Nano pivoted to the glass-stem log form factor [^1].
- Superseded by the [E-Nano NXT](../devices/TED-0010.md) (c. 2024), which reworked the thermals and added a weighted base [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer guidance (E-Nano Quick Start Guide, applies across the family — the guide does not distinguish OG from NXT/XL): never use alcohol- or citrus-based cleaners on the wood body, as they damage the natural shellac finish; the unit is designed to be run 24/7; never leave a glass stem resting on a hot heater port; black spots on the stem indicate combustion from too high a dial setting or too slow a draw [^6].
- The warranty is non-transferable and unit-specific [^4].
