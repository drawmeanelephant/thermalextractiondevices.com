## Revision Notes

- **v2 / 2.0 (c. 2012)**: one-click piezo ignition, faster butane fill valve, reduced gas waste; same 190 °C thermostat (reported) [^1].
- Superseded by the WISPR (Oct 2011), a wedge-form redesign with a viewing window and larger tank (reported) [^1].
- The IOLITE brand is reported to have ceased production in 2023, per the web-development agency that built its e-commerce site; this is why no live official product page exists to check for a part number (reported, single-source) [^4].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer/manual warnings: flammable gas under pressure, do not overfill, fill upright, wait 5 minutes if overfilled, and the device is not a medical device [^2].
