# Cannabis Hardware Airstream Vaporizer (TED-0033)

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Technical Specifications

| Property | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer | [Cannabis Hardware, LLC](../manufacturers/TMFR-0004.md) |
| Family | Airstream (integrated all-in-one — complete system) |
| Component Role ([TREF-0004](../reference/TREF-0004.md)) | Complete system — integrated desktop base station (3556) with built-in vapor path, integrated coil and controller, and stand; flower diffusers, bangers, and bowls are separate accessories (the "Airstream Essentials Cordless Ball Vape Kit" is a retail SKU, never a separate model) |
| Part Number | 3556 (base station); confirmed on the manufacturer's current product page, observed 2026-08-08 [^1][^6] |
| Release Period | 2024-11 – present (per research dossier; product page does not state a release date) [^2] |
| Heating Method | Integrated wireless ball convection; heat supplied by a built-in coil inside the base station [^1][^4] |
| Temperature Control | Integrated temperature controller (built-in PID per research dossier); no external PID or coil setup required [^1][^2] |
| Temperature Range | PID-controlled; numeric range not published by the manufacturer [^1] |
| Base Material | 6061 aircraft aluminum (per research dossier) [^2] |
| Dimensions | 5½" × 4" × 3" [^1] |
| Vapor Path | Built-in vapor path connecting two 18.8 mm pockets; two threaded cleanout plugs at each end; accepts a glass tube for cooler hits [^1] |
| Accessory Pockets | Two 25 mm pockets (quartz inserts / flower heads), five 18 mm components (two part of the vapor path), five dabber pockets, two ¼-20 accessory ports [^1] |
| Compatible Media | Dry botanical flower and concentrates [^1] |
| Compatible Heads & Accessories | 18.8 mm bowls (glass, Shovelhead, Matrix), mouthpieces, whips, and bangers; Freeze Pipe Glycerin Coil [^1] |
| Power | AC mains (integrated; power cord included) [^1] |
| Warranty | 1-year on metal [^3] |

## Revision Notes

- **Rev 2025-04-02**: implemented a new assembly process that reduced assembly time dramatically (per manufacturer product page) [^1].
- **Kit Rev 2026-03-05**: the Essentials kit began shipping with the Vmax head (per manufacturer bundle page) [^4].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer safety instruction: DO NOT leave the device unattended while hot [^1][^4].
- All accessories — flower diffusers and bangers — are sold separately [^1].

## Related Devices

- Manufactured by [Cannabis Hardware, LLC](../manufacturers/TMFR-0004.md).
- Compatible heater heads documented by the manufacturer: the [VMAX](../devices/TED-0044.md) (part 3574), which the Airstream Essentials Kit bundles by its correct part number [^4], and the [Mercury](../devices/TED-0036.md) (part 3518), whose product page names the Airstream as a supported system [^5].
