## Family Context

- The Bliss is the third member of the second-generation ZenLeaf base-station lineup ([Whisper](../devices/TED-0029.md), [Nova](../devices/TED-0007.md), [Bliss](../devices/TED-0030.md), [Fusion](../devices/TED-0031.md)); it is the only station in the collection with an external XLR output that powers a wired head separately from the ZenLeaf diffusers [^1].
- The base-station architecture superseded the first-generation [Mary](../devices/TED-0026.md)/[Jane](../devices/TED-0027.md) cordless diffusers (c. 2022) [^1][^2].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer ZenLeaf warnings apply: do not leave the station unattended while hot; the base and heated heads are burn hazards [^1][^3].
