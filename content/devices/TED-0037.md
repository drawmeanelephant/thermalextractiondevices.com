## Family Context

- The Showerhead was sold as a complete "Showerhead Assembly" (SKU 7005: Showerhead top SKU 3063 + FlowerPot 20 mm Coil Cover SKU 3061 + 20 mm Coil Nut SKU 3066, coil not included) and also as the top alone (SKU 3063) [^1][^2].
- Manufacturer description (Showerhead Assembly, 2019-12-15 snapshot): "Already a FlowerPot owner? Upgrade your rig to the latest FlowerPot head—the ShowerHead. Preferred by flower only vaping, the 19 intake air holes heat your air to the perfect temperature. Being versatile to dab on the titanium surface as well, this head offers the ultimate twax experience. The FlowerPot ShowerHead is the heart of the FlowerPot system." [^1]
- Manufacturer description (top-only SKU 3063, 2019-07-21 snapshot): "Experience the massive airflow of the ShowerHead Twax Vape, the ideal choice for flower enthusiasts and concentrate loves [lovers] alike. Capable of micro-dosing your flower, you'll be truly astonished at how much effect you can get from .05g!" — the listing also notes "Showerhead top only, no other parts included" [^2].
- **Relationship to the Vrod (TED-0038):** both are pre-ball FlowerPot heads sold in the same NewVape-era catalog. This archive found no manufacturer statement establishing which one preceded the other. Wayback image-cache assets tagged "showerhead" appear as early as 2017-10-16, while the earliest Vrod-specific asset found is dated 2019-01-30 — suggestive but **not conclusive** of the Showerhead predating the Vrod, since Wayback crawl depth varies independently of true release order. Because this cannot be confirmed from primary sources, the two are linked here only as siblings (`relates_to`), not as a `supersedes` chain in either direction. See the report for the full reasoning; this is a deliberate departure from the worker brief's assumed "Showerhead → Vrod" ordering, which is not supported by the cited evolution blog (that post starts from the Vrod and never mentions the Showerhead) [^6].
- The 19-hole air-inlet design is a direct predecessor of the wording later reused for the [FlowerPot B1](../devices/TED-0004.md), whose manufacturer product page also describes "19 drilled air-inlet holes" — the Showerhead's hole count and airflow-first design language persisted into the current ball-vape generation even though the underlying mechanism (solid plate vs. ball bed) changed completely.

## Safety Notes

- No Showerhead-specific safety warnings were found in the archived listings. General FlowerPot guidance applies by manufacturer practice at the family level: the heated head is a burn hazard, and hot titanium should not be left unattended or handled without protection. This is carried over from later FlowerPot pages, not confirmed on a Showerhead-specific page — labelled here as **community/family-level guidance**, not a Showerhead-specific manufacturer statement.
