## Family Context

- Manufacturer description (Vrod Head Assembly product page, both the 2020-08-13 NewVape-branded and 2021-04-18 Cannabis Hardware–branded snapshots use identical wording): "Vape Flower and Concentrates separate or together. One Large Air Hole Feeds 18 Smaller Holes. Grade 2 Titanium. Made In The USA. Requires 20mm Coil with 5 wraps in Clockwise direction (refer to photos). Includes: 28mm SiC Dish (9264), Vrod Head Top (3129), 18 Hole Diffuser (3142), Flowerpot Dish Nut (3010)." [^1][^2]
- **Price history (same SKU 7003, same description, same kit contents):** $115.00 on the 2020-08-13 newvape.com snapshot; $140.00 on the 2021-04-18 cannabishardware.com snapshot — a manufacturer-side price increase that coincides with the NewVape → Cannabis Hardware brand transition [^1][^2].
- **Sibling head:** the [Showerhead](../devices/TED-0037.md) was sold in the same NewVape-era catalog. This archive found no manufacturer statement establishing ordering between the two (see the Showerhead record for the evidence and reasoning) [^8].
- **Sibling head not modeled as an entity:** NewVape's own blog ("Vrod or WeedEater?", dated October 6, 2020) describes the "Weedeater" as "pretty much a Vrod with a dish-less top" — a flower-only, budget-tier variant of the Vrod. No archived standalone product page for a Weedeater head was located during this search pass (see the report's search-coverage section); it is not modeled as an entity here and is noted only as manufacturer-described context for the Vrod [^5].
- **Community modification — the "B-rod Mod" — is documented here as prose only, per this archive's rule that a customer modification is never a product entity.** Cannabis Hardware's own blog post introducing the ball-vape head (dated August 31, 2021) states: "If you have been following Troy @ 420 VapeZone then you have probably seen the 'B-Rod modification' which is basically the Vrod head filled with tons of little spherical rubies... Filling the head helps the Vrod retain heat and thus allows for vapor production at a lower temperature on the controller... Additionally, customers have been filling their personal vaporizers with balls and modifying them by themselves for years." The manufacturer's later evolution blog (2021-10-20) confirms the same origin: "It all started with the initial b-rod mod featured on 420vapezone and the Cannabis Hardware discord. Customers began modifying their own vrods by filling them with rubies... then used a screen to block off the top near the nut." Cannabis Hardware then formalized this into its own factory diffuser, the [FlowerPot Ball Vape](../devices/TED-0039.md) [^3][^9].

## Safety Notes

- No Vrod-specific safety warnings were found in the archived listings. General FlowerPot guidance applies by manufacturer practice at the family level: the heated head (including the SiC/sapphire dish) is a burn hazard and should not be left unattended while hot. This is carried over from later FlowerPot pages, not confirmed on a Vrod-specific page — labelled here as **community/family-level guidance**, not a Vrod-specific manufacturer statement.
