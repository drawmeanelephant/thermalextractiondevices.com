# Goboof Alfa (TED-0052)

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Technical Specifications

| Property | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer | [Goboof Products Limited](../manufacturers/goboof.md) |
| Release | ~Dec 2014 (early revision); "late/Classic" revision ~mid-2015 onward [^1][^3] |
| Heating Method | Conduction — resistive heater embedded in a hard-anodized aluminum oven with a central heating pin [^2] |
| Operation Mode | Session |
| Temperature Control | Rotary dial with five detents: OFF, LO, MID, HI, A/P, A/T [^2] |
| Temperature Range | LO 190 °C / 374 °F; MID 210 °C / 410 °F; HI 220 °C / 428 °F (per official manual); one reviewer records HI as 230 °C / 446 °F — discrepancy noted, not resolved [^2][^3] |
| Power Source | Internal non-user-replaceable lithium-ion battery; ~2 hours on the low setting [^2] |
| Charging | Proprietary DC barrel jack + wall adapter (early revision); USB Micro-B + wall adapter (late revision); ~4–4.5 h full charge; pass-through supported after ~20 % charge [^2][^3] |
| Dimensions | 87 × 48 × 19 mm (3.43 × 1.89 × 0.74 in) [^3] |
| Weight | ~100–103 g [^3] |
| Chamber Material | Hard-anodized aluminum [^2] |
| Compatible Media | Dry herb only (ground) [^2] |

**Revisions.** Two documented hardware revisions exist: an early revision (white logo, proprietary barrel charger, air holes on the oven-door plate, looser dial) and the late/"Alfa Classic" revision (gray logo, USB Micro-B charger, no air holes on the door plate, stiffer dial). Both are covered by the same 2014 manual [^3].

## Warranty & Service

- 2-year limited warranty on workmanship and materials; battery degradation excluded [^2][^3].
- Non-user-serviceable; repairs to the factory (Ireland) or authorized distributor; post-2020 service responsibility reported as assumed by Castle D Enterprises Ltd (uncorroborated against the CRO register — see the [Goboof manufacturer record](../manufacturers/goboof.md)).

## Cleaning & Maintenance Protocol

- Per the official manual: the oven gets very hot — allow cooling before handling; do not dismantle; use only the supplied Goboof charger [^2].
