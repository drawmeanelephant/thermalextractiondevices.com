## Revision Notes

- The Cloud EVO supersedes the Cloud (Gen 1, 2012) and the interim Cloud+ (~2013): Zytel housing (lighter, cooler base), faster CPU-controlled heater, airflow restriction moved to the bottom intake, single-hashmark dial, and a removable IEC C13 cord [^1].
- The Hanu Petra (Gen 3) was announced in 2021 but never shipped; the company ceased operations ~October 2025 before fulfillment [^2].

## Safety & Use Notes

- 110 V units are for USA/Canada/Mexico only; the manufacturer/retailer guidance states not to use voltage converters [^1][^4].
- The top of the unit above the dial gets hot during extended use [^1].
