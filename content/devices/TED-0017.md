## Revision Notes

- The Elite II supersedes the G Pen Elite (2016): hybrid heating, clean-air intake, zirconia mouthpiece, TFT display, USB-C, larger battery, and Wi-Fi firmware updating (reported) [^1].
- The Elite line is the second dry-herb product family from Grenco Science (after the G Pro Herbal / G Pen Pro line) (reported) [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer guidance (official FAQ): power off and let the device cool down before cleaning; after cleaning with isopropyl alcohol, run a session at maximum temperature to burn off residual ISO before normal use [^2].
- Battery is a 2100 mAh internal lithium-ion cell charged via USB-C; the manufacturer states roughly 60 minutes of heating time per full charge [^2].
