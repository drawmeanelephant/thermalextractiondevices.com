## Revision Notes

- The Elite II supersedes the G Pen Elite (2016): hybrid heating, clean-air intake, zirconia mouthpiece, TFT display, USB-C, larger battery, and Wi-Fi firmware updating (reported) [^1].
- The Elite line is the second dry-herb product family from Grenco Science (after the G Pro Herbal / G Pen Pro line) (reported) [^1].
