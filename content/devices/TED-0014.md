## Generation & Revision Notes

- **Gen 1 — High-Temp Cap** (c. 2015–2016): revision with a thicker heat-diffuser plate to resist warping at high temperatures; otherwise same architecture [^1][^4].
- **Gen 2 — INHALE era** (2023–present): production moved from California to Italy after Mendocino Therapeutics' owner retired; stainless steel chamber, revised 14.5 mm water-pipe adapter, glass J-Hook, semi-hard carrying case, and reengineered air-intake nozzles [^1][^5].
- The designer, Mark Krietzman, holds patents for electronic zoned convection vaporizers (e.g., US9826780B2) — those patents describe a different, battery-powered device, not the butane Lotus. No public patent specifically covering the Lotus flame cap was located [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Manufacturer/period guidance: keep flame length at or below 5/8 inch (≈½–5/8 in optimal); a longer (~1 in) flame risks combustion [^1][^6].
- No electronics, no firmware, no batteries; temperature control is entirely user technique [^1].
