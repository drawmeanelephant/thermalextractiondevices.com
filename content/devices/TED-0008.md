## Revision & Variant Notes

- **TinyMight 1.5** was a community-designated transitional run of the OG; the manufacturer never used the "1.5" designation. The TM2 is the first official generational change [^1].
- **TinyMight 2 Purple Heart** (2023–2024, limited): purpleheart wood, zirconia ceramic heater lining, reinforced heater support rods [^1][^4].
- **TinyMight 2 Black Edition** (Spring 2026): black-stained oak/redwood/walnut, redesigned solid-metal cooling unit, double-woven heater screen, external charger and spare battery included [^1].
- Heater-lining transitions (quartz glass → stainless steel → zirconia ceramic) are documented by community teardowns; the factory has not published serial breakpoints [^4].

## Safety & Use Notes

- The dial's "10" position ("beast mode") is described by third-party reviewers as an on-demand boost; concentrates are not an officially supported media for the standard configuration [^3].
- Device is fully user-serviceable (replaceable battery, removable stems); see the official manual for cleaning and calibration [^1].
