## Revision Notes

- The Switch² supersedes the original Switch (2018): fixed 3000 mAh battery (vs removable pack), 20 mm quartz cup (vs ceramic induction cups), IR temperature sensing, app control, and 6 dynamic modes [^1].
- The Switch GO (2026) is a compact derivative with dual-parallel heating and a 14 mm quartz insert [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Induction heats any conductive material — do not place metal objects in the glass top [^2].
- Manual cites over-temperature/voltage/current/short-circuit/reverse-polarity protection and a 15-minute auto shutoff [^2].
