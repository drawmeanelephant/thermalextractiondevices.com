## Family Context

- The MOAB is the third-generation ZenLeaf platform (2024-04), a compact follow-on to the second-generation base stations ([Whisper](../devices/TED-0029.md), [Nova](../devices/TED-0007.md), [Bliss](../devices/TED-0030.md), [Fusion](../devices/TED-0031.md)); the manufacturer's Essentials kit pairs it with a Vmax cordless head, Shovelhead bowl, and 25 mm quartz banger [^1][^2].
- **Coil note:** the manufacturer product page and current ZenLeaf collection state the MOAB is built around a **25 mm axial coil**; an earlier research dossier listing of a "16 mm coil" for the MOAB is superseded by the primary source [^1][^4].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer safety instruction: DO NOT leave the device unattended while hot [^1].
- A glass bong or water piece is required and sold separately [^1].
