## Family Context

- The MOAB is the third-generation ZenLeaf platform (2024-04), a compact follow-on to the second-generation base stations ([Whisper](../devices/TED-0029.md), [Nova](../devices/TED-0007.md), [Bliss](../devices/TED-0030.md), [Fusion](../devices/TED-0031.md)); the manufacturer's Essentials kit pairs it with the [Mercury](../devices/TED-0036.md) flower diffuser, a Shovelhead bowl, and a 25 mm quartz banger [^1][^2].
- **Bundled-head part-number correction:** the Essentials Kit prose states the bundle "pairs the MOAB with the Mercury Flower Diffuser," while the same page's "Bundle Includes" list names that component "VMAX Cordless Ball Vape with 3mm Rubies and Cocobolo handle (3518)" [^1]. Part **3518 is the Mercury**, listed by the manufacturer from 2024-04-02 — eighteen months before the [VMAX](../devices/TED-0034.md) (part 3574) was listed on 2025-09-23 [^7][^8]. The bullet-list label is a manufacturer copy error; the bundled head is the Mercury. Earlier revisions of this record repeated the erroneous "Vmax" attribution.
- **Coil note:** the manufacturer product page and current ZenLeaf collection state the MOAB is built around a **25 mm axial coil**; an earlier research dossier listing of a "16 mm coil" for the MOAB is superseded by the primary source [^1][^4].
- The MOAB shares its shielding concept with the [Zion](../devices/TED-0041.md) station (Zion omits the MOAB's integrated PID and adds dual 20 mm/25 mm coil compatibility) [^5][^6].

## Revision Notes

- **"X Pattern" standalone listing, 2025-10-08 (published 2025-10-09):** the manufacturer published the MOAB station as a standalone product for the first time — "Moab 'X Pattern' Wireless Ball Vape Station" (Shopify product 3583, variant SKUs 3506-110v / 3506-220v, $357) [^5]. The listing describes "the same dependable function you expect from the MOAB family with a fresh visual treatment and built-in billet heat shield" and "[i]ntegrated billet heat shield and 25 mm axial coil in a heat-shielded base" [^5]. Dimensions (5.5" × 4" × 2.75"), material (6061 aluminum, assembled in Florida, USA), and warranty (1-year, parts and labor) in the standalone listing match the figures already documented above for the MOAB station [^1][^5]. Whether "integrated billet heat shield" describes new hardware or is a copy clarification of the station's existing heat-shielded base is not resolved by the primary sources available — this record treats it as the manufacturer's current descriptive term for the feature pending further evidence, not as a confirmed hardware change.
- **Part-number relationship (documented, not asserted as separate products):** part number 3506 predates the standalone listing — the 2024-04 MOAB Essentials Kit page names the bundled station "MOAB Wireless Ball Vape 'XPattern' (3506)" [^1]. The 2025-10-08 standalone product is Shopify product 3583, with 3506 continuing as the prefix of its two variant SKUs (3506-110v, 3506-220v) [^5]. 3583 and 3506 are not competing or separate models: 3583 is the standalone listing's own product number; 3506 is the station's underlying part number, used both inside the 2024-04 kit bundle and as the 2025-10-08 standalone listing's variant SKU prefix.
- **Revision-vs-new-entity call:** documented here as an in-place revision of TED-0032, per the default in this archive's revision-modeling precedent (e.g., the ZenLeaf Rev 2 heat-deflector plate) and because the standalone listing shares the MOAB's family name, 25 mm axial coil, built-in PID, dimensions, material, and warranty with only a restyled visual treatment and a billet-heat-shield description as the delta [^1][^5]. See the worker report (`reports/zion-260808-1847-worker.md`) for the full reasoning; if the integrator's broader evidence changes this call, TED-0032 should be split rather than this note being taken as final.

## Safety Notes

- A glass bong or water piece is required and sold separately [^1].
