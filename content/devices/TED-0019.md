## Revision Notes

- **Rev A (2009–2011)**: fixed non-replaceable screen, no push-back ring, friction lid (reported).
- **Rev B (2011–2014)**: added rubber push-back ring, click-lock lid, improved screen fold (reported).
- **Rev C (2014–present)**: refined screen tension and standardized premium wood options [^1].
- Wood and finish variants (Cherry, Walnut, Monocle magnifier lid, Monocle Noir) are **cosmetic SKUs on the same chassis — not hardware generations** [^1].

## Safety & Use Notes

- A slow draw can risk combustion; the Flight Guide warns that battery overheating indicates the batteries should be replaced [^2].
- The box draws high current (~15 A class); only high-drain NiMH cells are appropriate [^2].
