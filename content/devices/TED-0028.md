## Revision & Discontinuation Notes

- The B2 follows the Screen Baller in the third-generation FlowerPot lineage; the manufacturer describes it as "its own ecosystem" with a custom dish nut for maximum interior ball space and a carb cap crowdsourced from the community Discord [^2].
- The [B1](../devices/TED-0004.md) is "a mix between the B-2 and the weedeater" — the flower-only variant that dropped the concentrate dish [^2].
- **Why it was discontinued (per manufacturer):** the B2 was originally designed to vaporize flower and concentrates at similar temperatures, but ruby ball media lowered flower vaporization temperatures enough to create a large flower/concentrate temperature delta that made true dual-purpose use impractical; running concentrates through ruby-filled heads also created significant cleaning and maintenance challenges. The manufacturer shifted to the B1 as the dedicated flower platform and to MOAB/Airstream for multi-purpose flexibility [^1]. No discontinuation date is manufacturer-published. A community/dossier estimate of "c. 2024" exists but is not treated as fact here [^5].
- **On the release date:** the product record behind today's bundle page traces back through several Shopify handles (`flowerpot-b2-head-assembly`, `flowerpot-b2-essentials-bundle`, `flowerpot-b2-premium-bundle`, `b2-injector-assembly`) crawled by the Wayback Machine between 2021-10-22 and 2023-03-21; the earliest of these — captured 2021-10-22, then titled "B-2 Head Assembly" — carries the manufacturer's own text "Introduced in Aug-2021," describing the head (at that point still called "the Baller") as machined from the Vrod head to accommodate rubies [^6]. This is consistent with, and slightly more precise than, the evolution blog's 2021-10-20 description of the B2 as "then in prototyping" [^2].
- **Coil note:** unlike the B1 (any standard 20 mm coil), the B2 requires the CH 20 mm coil — the machined ramp inside the head makes full contact with the coil's ramp for heat transfer [^3].
- **Bundle note:** the B2 was sold as a "Standard Essentials Bundle" (head assembly 7006, 4 mm ruby balls, universal carb cap, Shovelhead bowl, titanium screen, 14/18 mm posts, threaded heat stand, insulated heat post, enail handle, 20 mm coil, CH or Auber PID). Per TREF-0004, that bundle is a retail SKU of the B2 model — never a separate entity [^1].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer FlowerPot guidance applies: the heated head is a burn hazard; park the hot head on its stand and do not leave it unattended while hot [^3][^4].
- Do not exceed the 800 °F operating limit; the manufacturer recommends cleaning via the disassembly and cleaning manual rather than burn-offs [^3].
