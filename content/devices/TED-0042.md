## Family Context

- The Pulse product page states it is "designed to work with the traditional 20mm coil found in the Flowerpot ecosystem," and that combining it with the Clampy coil post "creates a complete cordless setup using your existing Flowerpot equipment" [^1]. This is FlowerPot (20 mm coil) evidence, not ZenLeaf (25 mm axial coil) evidence, even though Shopify's own `tags` field on this product is `zenleaf` [^1][^2]. This page classifies Pulse under the FlowerPot 20 mm platform on the strength of the manufacturer's body copy and documents the Shopify tag as a cataloging inconsistency rather than following it.
- Pulse pairs with [Swift](../devices/TED-0043.md) as the 18 mm/14 mm injector-joint pair on the same cordless-conversion concept, echoing the earlier Mary/Jane (female/injector) pairing on the ZenLeaf 25 mm platform — but Pulse and Swift are a 20 mm-coil pair, not a 25 mm-coil pair [^1].
- The variant SKU suffixes encode add-on components rather than distinct heads: `9465`/`9467` are handle choices (Cocobolo/Basswood), `3503(2)`/`9375(2)` are screen choices (machined/mesh, ×2), and `9422(3mm)` is the ruby-ball add-on. The base head (SKU 3516) is the entity modeled here; the options are accessories layered onto it, not separate models [^1].

## Safety Notes

- General Cannabis Hardware warnings apply to hot heater heads: do not leave a heated head unattended, and avoid exceeding manufacturer-recommended operating temperatures [^5].
