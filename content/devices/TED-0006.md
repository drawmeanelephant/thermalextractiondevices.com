## Revision Notes

- **Rev A (2023-06-07)**: fixed head seating in the F16 machined cap (per manufacturer product page) [^2].
- The F16 (injector) and F22 (diffuser) launched together in June 2023 as the fourth-generation FlowerPot heads [^1][^2].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer safety instructions for the F16 state: do not operate above 700 °F, do not perform burn-offs, and do not leave the unit unattended while hot [^2].
- The ruby balls must exceed the brim of the head for cap pressure; verify seating per manufacturer instructions [^2].
