## Family Context

- Jane is the injector (male) diffuser of the first-generation ZenLeaf pair (Mary/Jane, c. 2022); the base-station architecture (Whisper, Nova, Bliss, Fusion, 2023-09) superseded the generation-1 heads [^1].
- Mary and Jane are not listed in the current ZenLeaf collection as of 2026-08-08 (verified directly: the manufacturer's live ZenLeaf collection endpoint returns zero products, and no live product page exists under any tried Mary/Jane handle); the current diffuser lineup (e.g., Vmax, Vmax Injector) continues the 25 mm axial-coil platform [^5].
- The [ZenLeaf Nova](../devices/TED-0007.md) base station accepts Mary/Jane-style cordless diffusers [^1].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer ZenLeaf warnings apply: the base and heated heads are burn hazards; do not leave the station or a hot head unattended [^1][^3].
