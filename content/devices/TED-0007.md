## Family Context

- The Nova is the second-generation ZenLeaf base; the first generation (Mary and Jane cordless diffusers, c. 2022) were superseded by the base-station architecture [^1][^2].
- Sibling stations: Whisper (external PID, 2 pockets), Bliss (built-in PID plus external XLR output, 5 pockets), Fusion (dual PID, dual coils) [^1].

## Safety Notes

- Manufacturer warnings: do not leave the station unattended while hot; the base and heated heads are burn hazards [^3].
- The heat shield has two mounting positions; position per manufacturer instructions before use [^3].
