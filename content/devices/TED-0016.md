## Revision Notes

- The Boost EVO supersedes the Boost Black Edition (2017): closed-loop embedded temperature sensor (vs timed open-loop cycles), magnetic quartz eChamber, six presets, USB-C, and Bluetooth app support (reported) [^1].
- The XS (2023) is a compact EVO derivative with four presets (475–625 °F), spill-proof glass, and no app (reported) [^1].
- Serial-number-dependent fault codes distinguish early (02) and later (03+) EVO units in the official knowledge base [^2].

## Safety & Use Notes

- Official knowledge base: 15-minute auto shutoff, over-voltage and short-circuit protection, and a warning against high-wattage laptop chargers [^2].
