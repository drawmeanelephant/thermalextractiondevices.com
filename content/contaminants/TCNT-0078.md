**Class**: heavy metal

**Action limit**: ≤ 0.5 µg/g (ppm)
**Matrix**: All product categories (inhalable)

**Source**: CRA Technical Guidance for Laboratories 5.2, §4.6; R 420.304(1)(f)

**Testing requirement**: [[requirements/TREQ-0008|Heavy Metals]]
