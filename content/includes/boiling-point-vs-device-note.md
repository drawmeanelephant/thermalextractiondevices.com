> [!NOTE]
> **Boiling point is not a device setting.** A boiling point is a physical reference property of the pure compound, measured under stated conditions (normally the standard atmospheric pressure of 101.325 kPa, or a stated reduced pressure). It is not a prescription for a vaporizer temperature, and a device set to that temperature will not selectively extract only this compound. Vaporization from real plant material depends on partial vapor pressure, moisture, airflow, and thermal conductance, so device settings must be established empirically, per device and per load.
