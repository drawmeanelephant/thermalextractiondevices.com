# Public Health and Safety Advisory: Potentially Contaminated Marijuana Products

## Advisory Facts

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Advisory date | 2025-02-03 |
| Canonical URL | [official notice](https://masscannabiscontrol.com/2025/02/notice-massachusetts-cannabis-control-commission-public-health-and-safety-advisory-potentially-contaminated-marijuana-products-sold-between-september-18-2024-and-january-30-2025-february-3/) |
| Concern / reason | The affected products previously passed required compliance testing between August 2024 and September 2024; however, following additional compliance testing, samples of the affected products were found to have the presence of yeast and mold above the acceptable testing limits established for Total Yeast and Mold. |
| Affected-product count | 4 |
| Revision status | as published by the Commission |
| Source provenance | CCC Public Health and Safety Advisories portal |
| Sold between | 2024-09-18 and 2025-01-30 |

## Consumer Instructions

<Aside kind="warning">

Consumers that possess any of the affected products may destroy it or return it to the store where they purchased it for proper disposal.

</Aside>
