## Consumer Instructions

<Aside kind="warning">

Consumers and Patients that possess any of the affected products may destroy them or return them to the licensee where they were purchased for disposal and/or contact them for more information.

</Aside>

<Aside kind="info">

The Commission uses **public health and safety advisory** as the official term for these notices. This archive preserves that terminology and does not relabel the notice as a recall.

</Aside>
