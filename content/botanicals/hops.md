# Hops (*Humulus lupulus*)

## Taxonomic Identity

- **Kingdom**: Plantae
- **Family**: Cannabaceae
- **Genus**: *Humulus*
- **Species**: *H. lupulus*

## Shared Terpene Chemistry

Hops and cannabis are phylogenetic sister genera within the family *Cannabaceae*. Hops glandular trichomes synthesize an essential oil profile rich in identical volatile terpene compounds:

- [β-Myrcene](../terpenes/beta-myrcene.md)
- [β-Caryophyllene](../terpenes/beta-caryophyllene.md)
- [α-Humulene](../terpenes/alpha-humulene.md)

## Botanical Research & Reference

Lupulin glands on female hop cones produce essential oils historically utilized in brewing and herbal sleep preparations.
