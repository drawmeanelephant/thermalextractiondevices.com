# Lavender (*Lavandula angustifolia*)

## Taxonomic Identity

- **Kingdom**: Plantae
- **Family**: Lamiaceae
- **Genus**: *Lavandula*
- **Species**: *L. angustifolia*

## Shared Terpene Chemistry

Lavender essential oil contains high proportions of volatile monoterpenoids:

- [Linalool](../terpenes/linalool.md)
