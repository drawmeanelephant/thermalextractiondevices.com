## Overview

The Department of Cannabis Control (DCC) is issuing this notice of voluntary recall for multiple infused pre-roll products packaged by The Higher Path due to incomplete and incorrect regulatory compliance testing. These products have been sold under various brand names, including but not limited to The Artist Tree, Sweet Flower, and MMD.

## Consumer Action

If you purchased this product: check your package for the UID and batch number(s)
referenced in the official notice. If the numbers match, dispose of the product or
return it to the retailer for proper disposal. Contact a physician immediately if
you experience symptoms or adverse reactions.
