## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method**: True convection via the PerpetuHeat™ thermal system — a ceramic heater wrapped around a borosilicate glass "Bamboo" air path; the EVO added CPU-controlled temperature regulation [^1][^3].
- **Temperature Control**: Analog radial dial (200–500 °F range by clock-position reference); no user-updatable firmware [^1].
- **Power**: AC mains; removable IEC C13 cord on the EVO (110 V NA / 220 V EU units not interchangeable) [^1].
- **Chamber**: Stainless steel EZ Load Bowls (ELB); VapeXNail for concentrates [^1].
- **Certifications**: UL-certified heating element; CE for 220 V EU models [^1][^4].
- **Recalls**: None documented [^5].
