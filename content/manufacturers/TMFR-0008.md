Lineage and transition details above are from the Lotus research corpus artifact and INHALE official pages [^1][^2].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method**: Pure convection; a butane jet torch heats a nickel plate in a lotus-flower geometry, and drawn air carries heat into the chamber [^1][^2].
- **Temperature Control**: Manual flame technique (duration, distance, draw speed) — no electronics, no firmware [^1].
- **Materials**: Hardwood caps and shells; stainless steel chamber (Gen 2) [^1].
- **Certifications / Recalls**: None documented for either era (non-electronic device class) [^3].
