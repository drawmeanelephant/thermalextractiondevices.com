## Product Families

Cannabis Hardware's catalog is organized into three desktop architectures: wired ball-vape heads driven by a 20 mm enail coil and PID controller (FlowerPot), cordless wireless ball-vape base stations with built-in or external PID (ZenLeaf), and the integrated all-in-one Airstream unit [^3][^4].

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method**: Ball-assisted convection; air passes through a bed of 3 mm or 4 mm ruby (occasionally sapphire/quartz/SiC) balls heated by an enail coil [^2][^3].
- **Temperature Control**: External or integrated PID controllers (CH PID, Auber RDK 300A/300B); manufacturer FAQ lists a 550–800 °F operating range for wired heads, with recommended set points (e.g., 580–600 °F for B0) published per model [^5].
- **Heat Source**: 20 mm enail coil (FlowerPot), 25 mm axial coil (ZenLeaf), 16 mm coil (MOAB), or integrated coil (Airstream) [^4][^5].
- **Chamber Materials**: Grade 2 titanium heads; B2 adds a SiC or sapphire dish; wireless bases are 6061 aircraft aluminum (per manufacturer) [^2][^4].
- **Certifications**: No CPSC, FDA, or state recalls located as of 2026-08-08; "Certified Materials" claims on product pages are not backed by published certificates on the manufacturer site [^9].

## Warranty & Service

- 1-year warranty on metal components; 30-day on coils; 1-year on CH PID controllers; glass excluded; warranty is non-transferable and requires shipping to Boynton Beach, FL [^7].
- No user-facing firmware update process is documented for the built-in PIDs [^9].
