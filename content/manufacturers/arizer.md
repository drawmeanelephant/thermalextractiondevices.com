# Arizer

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Arizer (Plamondon Enterprises Inc.) |
| Headquarters | Waterloo, Ontario, Canada |
| Operating Principle | All-Glass Stem Vaporization Systems |
| Foundation Year | 2006 |

## Official Product Families

- **Portable Units**: Solo, Solo II, Solo III, Air SE, Air MAX.
- **Desktop Units**: Extreme Q, XQ2, V-Tower.

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Ceramic convection heating element with isolated stainless/ceramic chamber.
- **Power Sources**: High-capacity internal lithium-ion battery packs or AC desktop power supply.
- **Vapor Path Contact**: Borosilicate glass stems; air draw bypasses internal electronics.

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 2-year limited warranty on electronics and heating elements (lifetime warranty on ceramic heating element on select models). Glass components excluded from warranty.
