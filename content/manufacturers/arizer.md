# Arizer

{{include includes/manufacturer-claim-note.md}}

## Corporate Identity & Origin

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Legal Entity | Arizer (Canada-based; third-party business records list the entity as 7111495 Canada Inc.) [^1] |
| Headquarters | Kitchener, Ontario, Canada (per official About page; arizer.com footer lists Waterloo, ON) [^1] |
| Operating Principle | All-Glass Stem Vaporization Systems |
| Foundation Year | 2005 (per official company history) [^1] |

## Official Product Families

- **Portable Units**: Solo III, Solo II MAX, Air SE, Air MAX. (Solo II is a prior model now in the Arizer "Vault".) [^2]
- **Desktop Units**: Extreme Q, XQ2, V-Tower. [^2]

## Engineering & Architecture

- **Primary Heating Methods**: Ceramic convection heating element with isolated stainless/ceramic chamber (per manufacturer).
- **Power Sources**: High-capacity internal lithium-ion battery packs or AC desktop power supply.
- **Vapor Path Contact**: Borosilicate glass stems; air draw bypasses internal electronics (per manufacturer).

## Repairability & Warranty Terms

- **Warranty Duration**: 2-year limited warranty on electronics; lifetime warranty on the ceramic heating element; battery covered for 1 year; glass components excluded. Desktop Extreme Q is covered for 3 years [^3].
