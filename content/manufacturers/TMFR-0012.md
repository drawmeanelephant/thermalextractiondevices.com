## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method (butane)**: Flameless catalytic butane combustion conducted into a stainless steel heater pin; fixed-temperature bimetallic thermostat [^1][^4].
- **Heating Method (WISPR E)**: Internal Li-ion battery with electronic preset temperature ramp (190 → 210 °C); silent operation [^1][^4].
- **Temperature Control**: Fixed set points (190 °C on IOLITE Original and WISPR; 210 °C on WISPR 2; 190→210 °C ramp on WISPR E) — not user-adjustable [^1].
- **Certifications**: CE (implied by EU market presence) and patents; no public declarations of conformity located [^5].
