## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method**: Hybrid conduction/convection/radiant heating from an external butane jet/torch lighter; no batteries or electronics [^1][^4].
- **Chamber**: Gold-plated copper pan; the 2.0 and Click generations use a user-replaceable pan (2.0 ~0.1 g; Click larger ~0.25 g, per manufacturer/retailer specs) [^1][^4].
- **Temperature Control**: Manual flame timing/technique on generations 1–3; the Click adds a passive bi-metal acoustic click sensor at the vaporization threshold (~180–190 °C community estimate; exact figure not published by the manufacturer) [^1][^4].
- **Materials**: Hardwood bodies (olive, cherry, walnut, padauk, amaranth/purpleheart, etc.); stainless steel vapor path with Simrell intercooler on the 2.0 and Click [^1][^4].
- **Certifications**: CE marking claimed for the Swiss-made non-electrical device; no FCC/UL (no electronics). No public certificate numbers located [^5].
