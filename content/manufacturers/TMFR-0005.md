## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method**: Pure on-demand convection; the TM2 uses a stainless steel spiral heater rated 70 W (per official site; some third-party reviews state 75 W) [^3][^4].
- **Temperature Control**: Analog 0–10 dial with half-steps (~20 settings) over 120–240 °C; TM2 adds user calibration and ±1 °C regulation [^3][^4].
- **Power**: Single user-replaceable 18650 cell (Molicel P28A/P30B recommended); micro-USB on the OG, USB-C on the TM2 [^3][^4].
- **Materials**: Oak/walnut/cherry wood bodies; glass-lined airpath (OG) vs quartz glass, stainless steel, or zirconia ceramic liner (TM2 runs) [^3].
- **Certifications**: Finnish Key Flag product mark for TM2 (per official EU store); CE and RoHS compliance claimed; "hand-built in Finland" is manufacturer marketing language [^3].
- **Recalls / Safety Notices**: None found in CPSC, EU RAPEX, or SaferProducts.gov databases as of 2026-08-08 [^5].
