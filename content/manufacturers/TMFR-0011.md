## Engineering & Architecture

- **Heating Method**: Conduction + infrared through a stainless steel mesh screen; no electronics in the box itself — power comes from AA NiMH batteries or the Power Adapter/Power Up line [^1][^4].
- **Temperature Control**: Manual draw-rate control on the Boxes; continuous dial on Power Adapter and Power Up units [^1].
- **Materials**: Solid hardwood bodies; 304 stainless steel screens; Pyrex draw stem [^1][^4].
- **Design Notes**: The Launch Box screen is non-user-replaceable (warranty exchange); the MD Dab Box screen is user-replaceable [^4].
- **Certifications / Recalls**: None listed (artisan, non-electronic device class) [^3].
